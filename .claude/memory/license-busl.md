On 2026-09-03 the owner moved the project's own code from MIT to the Business Source License 1.1 (issue #3068), after a talk with a company interested in buying and reselling FerroEHR under its own name that did not want MIT. Parameters: Licensor Ruben Talstra; Additional Use Grant written for a CDR (tightened the same day at the owner's direction: production use is free for Non-Commercial Purposes only, i.e. research, teaching, personal use, non-profit or public bodies outside the course of a business; any other production use incl. paid health care, a hosted/managed/embedded service for third parties, and for-fee distribution alone or inside another product need a commercial licence); Change Date four years per version; Change License Apache 2.0. Releases through v4.0.17 and crates through 0.0.56 stay MIT as published. Contributions carry an inbound relicensing grant (CONTRIBUTING.md) so the work stays one work under one licensor.

On 2026-09-04 the owner extended BUSL-1.1 to the three hand-written spec engines, `openehr-query`, `openehr-adl` and `openehr-its` (from crate version 0.0.60; 0.0.58 and 0.0.59 stay Apache-2.0), so a competitor cannot assemble a CDR from the published engines. Each ships its own `LICENSE` with the crate named as the Licensed Work. `openehr-its` declares `BUSL-1.1 AND Apache-2.0` and keeps `LICENSE-APACHE-2.0` for the openEHR-derived generated codecs, REST contract and embedded ITS-JSON schema; its headers carry both holders. Only the five generated model crates (`openehr-base`, `openehr-rm`, `openehr-am`, `openehr-lang`, `openehr-term`) remain Apache-2.0.

**Why:** the owner wants no resale and no SaaS by others without permission, and a buyer wants exclusivity and a named copyright holder. No OSI licence can express that (OSD criteria 1 and 6); BUSL beat Elastic 2.0 (allows resale of copies), FSL (two-year conversion), PolyForm Shield (permanent, low recognition), AGPL (does not stop SaaS). The engines were added because they are the project's own engineering, the pieces a clean-room CDR would lean on; the generated model has no such moat (the BMM is public) and keeps its community value.

**How to apply:** new first-party files carry `SPDX-FileCopyrightText: Cadasto B.V.` + `SPDX-License-Identifier: BUSL-1.1`, including `crates/openehr-query` and `crates/openehr-adl`; `crates/openehr-its` files carry both holders and `BUSL-1.1 AND Apache-2.0` (the codegen `spdx.rs` emitter and `scripts/checks/spdx-headers.sh` encode the three header shapes); files under the five generated crates and their codegen templates carry the Apache dual header. A licence change is never silent: it goes through the changelog, the book's licensing chapter, `REUSE.toml`, the crate READMEs and `deny.toml` in one PR (the `licensing-declarations.sh`, `spdx-headers.sh` and `packaged-attribution.sh` guards compare them). Never call the project open source, say source-available. The grant wording is the owner's legal text: propose changes, never edit it on your own. See [[owner-work-style]], [[sibling-products]].
