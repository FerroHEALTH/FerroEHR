EDPB reuse terms
================

This identifier covers the European Data Protection Board documents vendored
at `docs/law/eu/edpb-guidelines-01-2025-pseudonymisation/` and
`docs/law/eu/edpb-guidelines-02-2023-eprivacy-5-3/`, fetched by
`scripts/vendor/law-eu.sh`. It is not an SPDX-listed licence. It is the reuse
policy the EDPB states for its own material, recorded in machine-readable form
so that a glob elsewhere in REUSE.toml cannot assert the wrong terms over it.

https://www.edpb.europa.eu/copyright_en, read 2026-09-12 and read again

2026-10-05):

How it applies to this repository
---------------------------------

Each vendored file is the published PDF, reproduced byte for byte. The three
conditions are met and stay met by construction:

  * the source is acknowledged in each directory's `PROVENANCE.md` and in the
    index at `docs/law/README.md`;
  * the meaning is not distorted, because no document is converted,
    extracted from or paraphrased anywhere in this repository, and each SHA-256
    is recorded so that stays checkable;
  * no liability is imputed to the EDPB by anything here.

Each record also states what the document is: guidelines, which bind nobody.
