IETF Trust Legal Provisions
===========================

This identifier covers the IETF RFCs vendored at `docs/specs/rfc/`, fetched
by `scripts/vendor/rfcs.sh`. It is not an SPDX-listed licence. It is the
grant the IETF Trust makes over every IETF Document, recorded in
machine-readable form so that a glob elsewhere in REUSE.toml cannot assert the
wrong terms over them.

What each document states
-------------------------

Each RFC carries this Copyright Notice, with its own year:

  Copyright (c) <year> IETF Trust and the persons identified as the document
  authors. All rights reserved.

  This document is subject to BCP 78 and the IETF Trust's Legal Provisions
  Relating to IETF Documents (https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info) in
  effect on the date of publication of this document.

What the Trust Legal Provisions grant
-------------------------------------

Quoted verbatim from section 3.c of the Trust Legal Provisions 5.0, effective
2015-03-25, https://trustee.ietf.org/documents/trust-legal-provisions/tlp-5/,
read 2026-11-05. Section 3.c of TLP 4.0, effective 2009-12-28, which governs
the RFCs published before 2015-03-25, grants the same in the same words
(https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info/IETF-TLP-4.htm):

  c. Licenses For Use Outside the IETF Standards Process. In addition to the
  rights granted with respect to Code Components described in Section 4
  below, the IETF Trust hereby grants to each person who wishes to exercise
  such rights, to the greatest extent that it is permitted to do so, a
  non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide right and license under all
  copyrights and rights of authors:

    i. to copy, publish, display and distribute IETF Contributions and IETF
    Documents in full and without modification,

Every file under `docs/specs/rfc/` is the whole document, unmodified, with
every legend and notice it carries, which is what that grant covers.
