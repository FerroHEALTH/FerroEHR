IHE Technical Framework copyright licence
=========================================

This identifier covers the IHE documents vendored at `docs/specs/ihe-consents/`,
fetched by `scripts/vendor/ihe-consents.sh`: the Basic Patient Privacy
Consents (BPPC) chapters of the IT Infrastructure Technical Framework, the
Advanced Patient Privacy Consents (APPC) supplement, and the chapter quoted
below. It is not an SPDX-listed licence. It is the grant IHE International
states for its Technical Framework documents, recorded in machine-readable
form so that a glob elsewhere in REUSE.toml cannot assert the wrong terms
over them.

How this repository uses it
---------------------------

The documents are reproduced whole and unmodified, which is what the grant
permits. The chapter itself is vendored beside them as
