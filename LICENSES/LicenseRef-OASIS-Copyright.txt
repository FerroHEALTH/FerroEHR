OASIS copyright notice
======================

This identifier covers two OASIS Standards: the SAML V2.0 Profiles vendored
at `docs/specs/oasis-saml/`, fetched by `scripts/vendor/oasis-saml.sh`, and
the XACML 2.0 core specification vendored at `docs/specs/xacml/`, fetched by
`scripts/vendor/xacml.sh`. It is not an SPDX-listed licence. It is the grant
each document states in its Notices appendix, recorded in machine-readable
form so that a glob elsewhere in REUSE.toml cannot assert the wrong terms
over them.

Version 2.0", OASIS Standard, 1 Feb 2005,
