OpenID Foundation specification notice
======================================

This identifier covers OpenID Connect Core 1.0 vendored at
`docs/specs/openid-connect-core/`, fetched by
`scripts/vendor/openid-connect-core.sh`. It is not an SPDX-listed licence. It
is the grant the specification states in its Appendix C, Notices, recorded in
machine-readable form so that a glob elsewhere in REUSE.toml cannot assert the
wrong terms over it.

---------------------------
