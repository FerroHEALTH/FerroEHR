eHDSI document reuse statement
==============================

This identifier covers the eHDSI documents vendored at
`docs/specs/ehdsi-specifications/`, copied from the owner's retrieval by
`scripts/vendor/ehdsi-specifications.sh`. It is not an SPDX-listed licence. It
is the statement each document's cover and control page makes, recorded in
machine-readable form so that a glob elsewhere in REUSE.toml cannot assert the
wrong terms over them.

What the documents state
------------------------

Quoted verbatim from the cover and control page of each of the eHDSI
Interoperability Specifications 8.0.1, the SAML Profile 10.0.0, the Audit
Trail Profile 10.1.0, Section I Security Policies 10.0.0, Section II Security
Services 10.0.0 and the XCA Profile 8.0.1, read 2026-11-05:

  Reuse is authorised, provided the source is acknowledged.

  Distribution level*: PU

  * Distribution level: PU = Public, ...

How this repository uses it
---------------------------

The documents are copied verbatim, as the owner supplied them. The
acknowledgement is the tree's PROVENANCE.md: the documents are DG SANTE's,
the Directorate-General for Health and Food Safety of the European
Commission, published for MyHealth@EU, and each cover's source line names it.

The MyHealth@EU Requirements Catalogue 10.0.0 makes no such statement and is
not covered: no copy of it is in the repository.
