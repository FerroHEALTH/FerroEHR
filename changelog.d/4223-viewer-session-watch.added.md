- Every signed-in viewer page carries FerroUI's session watch: two minutes before the idle window ends a dialog offers Stay signed in or Sign out, typing or clicking on the page keeps the session going at most once a minute, and when the session has ended the page leaves for the sign-in page by itself, which returns to the page once you sign in again; every other viewer tab in the browser leaves with it, and signing out in one tab ends the others. The watch asks the new session route `/api/session`, whose reads never extend the session.
