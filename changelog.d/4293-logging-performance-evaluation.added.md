- The logging component's intended performance is stated as checkable claims (EHDS Annex II 1.1): every composition create and read recorded exactly once and every AQL execution at least once, no access succeeding without its record, at most 125 % of the latency and at least 80 % of the throughput with the access log off, and a 50-record ITI-81 page within 250 ms at p95. `scripts/perf/audit.sh` measures them with the access log off and on (the load test's new `--audit` mode counts the records through ITI-81 and times retrievals), `scripts/perf/audit-verdict.sh` gives the verdict, the release refuses a claim not met and attaches the summary as `ferroehr-<tag>-logging-performance.md` (Annex III point 2), and the weekly performance guard runs it on main. The book's compliance section gains the page that states the claims and the method.
