- `scripts/interop/atna.sh` exercises the logging component's ATNA transactions against independent implementations run from published images (EHDS Annex II 1.4): the ITI-20 FHIR feed into HAPI FHIR as the Audit Record Repository, judged by its validator with IHE BALP 1.1.4; the ITI-20 syslog feed into syslog-ng over RFC 5425 TLS, each payload read as a DICOM PS3.15 `AuditMessage`; and the same ITI-81 searches put to FerroEHR and to HAPI. The audit trail page documents it.
