- Retention periods and access rules can take the origin and the category of the data into account (EHDS Annex II 3.4). The new top-level `[origins]` register declares the origins a deployment recognises, each by the id its data carry and the clients whose direct commits come from it; a body without a `FEEDER_AUDIT` committed by a registered client is stamped with that client's origin instead of the server's `system_id`. `PUT /admin/retention/policy` takes an optional `origin` naming a registered origin, the register and `GET /admin/retention/due` report it, and the due list lists an origin-keyed period over the objects of that origin (clinical migration `0020_retention_by_origin.sql`). The ABAC request carries `category` (the access record's category tokens) and `origin` resource attributes on composition, contribution and query operations, the Cedar schema declares both, and a remote PDP binding may name them as parameters.
