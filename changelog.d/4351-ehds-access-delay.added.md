- The EHDS Art. 3(3) delay of the person's own access to newly registered data, a Member State option and off by default. The new `[access_delay]` section (`enabled`, `categories`, `hours`) holds every new COMPOSITION of a listed `[audit.categories]` category from requests that may be the person's own (a bearer token with a SMART `patient/` grant or the ABAC patient claim, also beside `user/` or `system/` grants, as `ferrohealth-smart` 0.8.7 decides it) for at most `hours`: a read answers `404` as for an unknown composition, and a query, an export and a CONTRIBUTION leave it out, while a professional reads it as before. The treating professional releases a held composition from the clinical route `POST {ext}/ehr/{ehr_id}/versioned_composition/{versioned_object_uid}/access-delay/release`, with the checks of a write to that EHR, and the controller with `POST /admin/access-delay/release`; a professional holds one by hand within the limit with `POST /admin/access-delay` and reads the register with `GET /admin/access-delay`; every hold ends at its limit, and placing, releasing and expiry are each an access record. The subject-scoped ITI-81 read of the person's own trail (EHDS Art. 9) leaves out the records about a held composition until it is released or expires, then serves them with their original times (`ehds-logging` 0.3.3) (clinical migration `0027_access_delay.sql`, party migration `0019_access_delay_column.sql`).
