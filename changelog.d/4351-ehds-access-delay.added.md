- The EHDS Art. 3(3) delay of the person's own access to newly registered data, a Member State option and off by default. The new `[access_delay]` section (`enabled`, `categories`, `hours`) holds every new COMPOSITION of a listed `[audit.categories]` category from patient-context requests (a SMART `patient/` grant or the ABAC patient claim) for at most `hours`: a read answers `404` as for an unknown composition, and a query, an export and a CONTRIBUTION leave it out, while a professional reads it as before. A health professional releases a held composition with `POST /admin/access-delay/release`, holds one by hand within the limit with `POST /admin/access-delay`, and reads the register with `GET /admin/access-delay`; every hold ends at its limit, and placing, releasing and expiry are each an access record (clinical migration `0027_access_delay.sql`, party migration `0019_access_delay_column.sql`).
