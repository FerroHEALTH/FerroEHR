- A version whose content equals its predecessor's stores that content once: the version row, its commit audit, its contribution, its attestations and its uid-stamped body are written as before, and the node rows are not, the new version reading its predecessor's through the new `version.content_sys_version` (migrations `clinical/0032`, `party/0025` and `clinical/0033`, which re-creates the `retention_due` view). Every reader, AQL included, answers each version with its own uid. The digest the content is compared on is `version.content_digest`, SHA-256 over the canonical body with the root uid removed; a row written before keeps its own rows and is never shared.
