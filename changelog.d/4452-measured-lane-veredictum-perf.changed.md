- The measured performance lane runs Veredictum 0.3.3's `perf`. `scripts/conformance.sh perf` composes the conformance deployment with the measurement overlay, runs the pinned `clinical-mix` workload, checks the record with `perf-judge` and redraws the book's charts from `docs/conformance/ferroehr/perf-result.json`. `scripts/conformance.sh perf-aa` measures the deployment against itself, so the record carries its between-run noise floor. The measurement overlay also switches response compression off, and the IXIT names the deployment's containers for the run's server-side readings. The Performance and Benchmarks pages describe the new instrument. They keep the earlier figures until a record measured on the reference host replaces them.
