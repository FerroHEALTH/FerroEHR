- The EHDS Art. 10 opt-out from access through the access services of Arts. 4 and 12, a Member State option and off by default. The new `[authz.rbac] access_service_roles` key declares the roles of the access-service callers; while an EHR carries the opt-out, a caller holding one reads it as absent: a route naming the EHR or an object in it answers `404` as for an unknown EHR, a population query leaves its data out, a scoped query, an export and the lookup by subject find no such EHR, and a write to it is refused as absent. Every other caller, the provider's own care included, reads it as before. The controller records the opt-out on the person's instruction with `POST /admin/access-opt-out`, reverses it with `POST /admin/access-opt-out/reverse` (the register keeps the reversal) and reads the register with `GET /admin/access-opt-out`; recording is refused with `400` while no access-service role is declared. An access-service caller that declares one of `[audit] emergency_purpose_codes` and does not act for the person reads the EHR under the Art. 10(2) vital-interest override, with the emergency mark on its access record and an `access-opt-out-override` record against the EHR. Recording, reversing and reading the register are access records (clinical migration `0029_access_opt_out.sql`).
