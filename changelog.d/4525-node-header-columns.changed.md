- A clinical or party node row below the root no longer repeats its archetype header, language and encoding: an archetype root's `archetype_details` and `archetype_node_id` are rebuilt from the `archetype` column and a new `rm_version` column, and a `language` in `ISO_639-1` and an `encoding` in `IANA_character-sets` keep only their code, in the new `language` and `encoding` columns. Every reader puts the keys back where they stood, so the canonical JSON, every AQL result and the served key order are unchanged; a header of any other shape stays in the fragment. A vital signs version takes 17.1 KB instead of 21.6 KB and an International Patient Summary 131 KB instead of 159 KB. Migrations `ext/0007`, `clinical/0028` and `party/0020` add the columns and the readers; no data is migrated, and a row written before keeps its whole fragment and reads the same.
