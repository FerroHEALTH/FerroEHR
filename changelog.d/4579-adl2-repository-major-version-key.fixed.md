- An ADL 2 template or archetype that references two major versions of one archetype (`….v1` and `….v2`), or one archetype under two namespaces, now compiles against both: the `openehr-adl` repository holds an artefact under its namespace, archetype and major version, and resolves each reference within that key (BASE Artefact Identification, Referencing). A reference without a namespace now resolves in the namespace of the artefact that makes it, and never in another namespace.
