- **Breaking:** FerroEHR no longer publishes change events to a message broker. The AMQP publisher, the `lapin` dependency, the `events` cargo feature, the `[events]` section, the `/admin/event_subscription` routes with their subscription store (clinical migration `0030_outbox_feed.sql` drops the table), the `events` health indicator and `events_published` metric, the `plaintext_broker` deployment gap, the chart's `secrets.eventsUrl` and `config.events` values, and the viewer's Subscriptions page are gone. A configuration that still carries `[events]`, or `plaintext_broker` in `deployment_accepts`, is refused at boot. Broker delivery moves to FerroBRIDGE, where it is in progress ([FerroHEALTH/FerroBRIDGE#1367](https://github.com/FerroHEALTH/FerroBRIDGE/issues/1367)): it is to read the new outbox feed and publish to the broker, so a deployment that needs broker events stays on a release with FerroEHR's own publisher until a FerroBRIDGE release carries it. Then name its reader in `[outbox] readers` and configure FerroBRIDGE's events lane with the broker URL, exchange and queues ([Change events (outbox feed)](https://ferroehr.eu/docs/latest/beyond-core/outbox-feed.html)).
