- **Breaking:** FerroEHR no longer publishes change events to a message broker. The AMQP publisher, the `lapin` dependency, the `events` cargo feature, the `[events]` section, the `/admin/event_subscription` routes with their subscription store (clinical migration `0030_outbox_feed.sql` drops the table), the `events` health indicator and `events_published` metric, the `plaintext_broker` deployment gap, the chart's `secrets.eventsUrl` and `config.events` values, and the viewer's Subscriptions page are gone. A configuration that still carries `[events]`, or `plaintext_broker` in `deployment_accepts`, is refused at boot. RabbitMQ users move delivery to FerroBRIDGE, which reads the new outbox feed and publishes to the broker: name its reader in `[outbox] readers` and configure FerroBRIDGE's events lane with the broker URL, exchange and queues ([Change events (outbox feed)](https://ferroehr.eu/docs/latest/beyond-core/outbox-feed.html)).
