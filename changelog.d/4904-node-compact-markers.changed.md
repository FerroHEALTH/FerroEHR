Four new boolean markers on the row (`origin_compact`, `time_compact`, `party_self`, `uid_derived`) say which keys it left out, so every reader puts them back exactly where they stood and a fragment of any other shape stays as written; the canonical JSON, every AQL result and the served key order are unchanged. Migrations `ext/0010`, `clinical/0031` and `party/0024` add the markers and the readers; no data is migrated, and a row written before keeps its keys and reads the same.
