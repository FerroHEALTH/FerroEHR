- The ADL 2 WebTemplate builder gives each `use_archetype` sibling of one archetype that the template names with a fixed `name/value` its own name-qualified path (`[openEHR-EHR-SECTION.adhoc.v1,'Medication summary']`), as the ADL 1.4 builder does, so a composition holding several such sections, such as an International Patient Summary, validates instead of being refused with "too many occurrences".
