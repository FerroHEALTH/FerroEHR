- A population AQL read ordered by the composition uid with a `LIMIT` (`SELECT … FROM EHR e CONTAINS COMPOSITION c CONTAINS OBSERVATION o[…] ORDER BY c/uid/value LIMIT 50`) stops at its limit again. Since a version identical to its predecessor reads its predecessor's node rows, the store holds fewer node rows, and the planner's estimate of the answer fell to a tenth of its size: it then joined the whole population into a sort, and the storage bench's flat two-leaf read took thirteen times the pages. The ADL 1.4 specialisation test of an archetype predicate is now a byte range the archetype extended statistics read (`arch_concept ~>=~ 'concept-' AND arch_concept ~<~ 'concept.'`, the same strings as `LIKE 'concept-%'`), and a node contained in a `COMPOSITION`, `EHR_STATUS` or `EHR_ACCESS` root joins it on `num > 0` in place of the root's nested-set interval.
