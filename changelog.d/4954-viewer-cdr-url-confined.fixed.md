- The viewer refuses, before sending it, a CDR request whose URL would leave the configured CDR: another scheme, host, port or user, a path outside the product root of `[cdr] base_path`, the management base or the health family, or a path that holds a `.` or `..` segment in any spelling, a `\` or a control character. A browser-supplied id, stored-query name or version of `..` was percent-encoded unchanged and resolved away from the ITS-REST base; it now draws the refusal instead. A `[cdr]` URL that makes no absolute URL stops the viewer at start.
