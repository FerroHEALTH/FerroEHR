- The login binding reads the local party source on every request, with no cache: the new party migration `0027_binding_side_table` adds the indexed `binding_identifier`, `binding_membership` and `binding_representation` relations, written in each party commit's transaction from the `PERSON`s of the mapped templates that perform a professional `ROLE` or represent another person, their `GROUP` memberships and their representation relationships, and rebuilt from the party versions alone. A login matching two parties, an `inactive`, `abandoned` or deleted party, a token without the key claim, a party without the needed marker and unreadable groups are refused with `403` and their binding code; a party store or identity provider that cannot answer is answered `503`.
