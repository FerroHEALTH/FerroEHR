- A person of a `persons` issuer whose login no demographic party carries is refused with `403` and `refused-unbound` on every request, with no fallback to a login-only committer. The problem names the identifier issuer to provision the person under, a token without the key claim is refused with a problem naming that claim, and each refusal counts into the new `binding_refusals` metric by issuer and outcome. The Keycloak quickstart overlay provisions its four demo users through SCIM with the `ferroehr-provision-keycloak` one-shot. Upgrade: provision every professional (through SCIM or the demographic API) before you add a `persons` issuer to the upgraded deployment.
