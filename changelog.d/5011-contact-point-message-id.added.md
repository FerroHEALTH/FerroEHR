- The access record of a MyHealth@EU contact point's request carries the
  request message's UUID, the WS-Addressing `MessageID` its token relays as
  `ehdsi.message_id`, as the record's `request-message-id`. The record then
  joins the contact point's Patient Privacy audit entry (eHDSI Audit Trail
  Profile 10.1.0 §2.3.12 to §2.3.32).
