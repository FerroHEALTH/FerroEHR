- With a party mapping configured, the parties the login binding relies on are written through the demographic API by an administrator (`authz.rbac.admin_role`) only: a `PERSON` of a mapped template carrying a binding key, `userName` or `externalId`, its professional `ROLE`, a representation relationship, and a `GROUP` of the group template. Any other caller's write is refused with `403`, a version that changes a present binding key is refused with `403`, and an administrator's version that leaves a protected identifier out is refused with `422`. Upgrade: write these parties as an administrator, and change a key through `POST /admin/party/binding-key`.
