- `[scim]` mounts the SCIM 2.0 service provider at `/scim/v2/` (off by default): `[[scim.client]]` declares each provisioning client by `issuer`, `client_id`, `scope` and `binding_issuer`, and `public_base_url` names the origin its documents hang off. `/scim/v2/ServiceProviderConfig` answers without a token; `/ResourceTypes` and `/Schemas` answer a declared client acting for nobody that holds its scope, within `[server.caller_rate]`. Every refusal is an RFC 7644 `Error` message. The start is refused for a client of a non-`machines` issuer, one declared twice, two clients sharing a `binding_issuer`, or a `binding_issuer` no `[[auth.party]]` table carries.
