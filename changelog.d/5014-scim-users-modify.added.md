- `/scim/v2/Users/{id}` takes `PUT`, `PATCH` and `DELETE`: a write commits the next version of the professional's `PERSON`, clearing a mapped attribute a `PUT` leaves out, keeping the binding key and `externalId`, and moving the lifecycle state by `active` (`complete` or `incomplete` to `inactive` or `abandoned` and back, an `abandoned` User completed in two versions, an attribute change to an inactive User refused until it is reactivated). `DELETE` logically deletes the `PERSON` and its professional `ROLE` in one CONTRIBUTION and leaves the binding key as a tombstone. An `If-Match` naming another version is `412`, and `ServiceProviderConfig` declares PATCH. `[scim] max_deactivations` and `deactivation_window_minutes` bound the deactivations and deletions of one client, counted in the database so replicas share the bound, with `429` past it.
