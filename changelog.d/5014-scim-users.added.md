- `/scim/v2/Users` serves a client's Users: `POST` creates the professional's `PERSON` and its professional `ROLE` in one demographic CONTRIBUTION, `complete` when the template holds in full and `incomplete` otherwise, `GET /Users/{id}` answers with the version's entity tag and `304` to a matching `If-None-Match`, and `GET /Users` answers a `ListResponse` filtered by `eq` and `and` over `userName`, `externalId` and `id`, with any other filter `400` `invalidFilter`. A client sees only the Users whose binding key its `binding_issuer` issues, every SCIM request is an access record of the client naming the `PERSON`, and `ServiceProviderConfig` declares entity tags. The start is refused when `[scim]` is on and the party mapping has no default person template with a `user_name` selector or no professional role.
