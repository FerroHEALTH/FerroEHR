- `/scim/v2/Groups` serves SCIM Groups as demographic `GROUP`s of `[authz.party_binding.group]`, each member holding an inline `PARTY_RELATIONSHIP` of `[authz.party_binding.membership]` from its `PERSON` to the `GROUP`, with no `ROLE` minted: create, read, `eq`/`and` filters on `displayName`, `externalId` and `id`, `excludedAttributes=members`, `PATCH` (answered `204`), `PUT` and `DELETE`, with the entity tags of Users. A Group belongs to the client that created it, its members are that client's Users, its `displayName` is unique, and its `externalId` sits under the client's `group_identifier_issuer`. A `remove` of `members` with a `value` list removes only those members, an Entra compatibility deviation from RFC 7644 §3.5.2.2. Deleting a User removes its memberships in the same CONTRIBUTION. `[scim] max_group_members` (default 5000) caps a Group.
