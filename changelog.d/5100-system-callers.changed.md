- A system caller (an app-only token by its issuer profile's `app_only` markers) holds `system/` scopes only: a token carrying a `user/` or `patient/` scope is refused with `403`. It commits into an EHR only as a client an `[[authz.system_client]]` table declares, under the committer `PARTY_IDENTIFIED{name = <client>, identifiers = [{issuer = <iss>, id = <client>, type = "client"}]}`, and each COMPOSITION it commits must be of a template its `writes` lists (`403`), name a facility its `facilities` lists in `context.health_care_facility`, or in `feeder_audit.originating_system_audit.location` when it has no context (`422`), and be either a device act from a `device` client or a relayed person act naming the professional by an identifier from a `relay` client. Upgrade: declare every client that commits in `[[authz.system_client]]` with its `writes`, `facilities` and `origins`, and give its compositions the facility identifier.
- An attestation is accepted only from a caller bound to a professional `PERSON`; one from a patient, a representative, a system caller or an unbound caller is refused with `403`. Upgrade: attest under the professional's own token.
- A guest of a `persons` issuer, by its profile's `guest` markers, is refused with `403` (`refused-guest`) unless the issuer's `[[auth.party]]` table sets `guests = true`. Upgrade: set `guests = true` on the issuers whose guests act under your responsibility.
