- Every refused request is an access record through the gated path, and a refusal of the login binding or of a caller kind carries its `refused-*` binding code and the party it names. Refusals of tokens that failed verification are budgeted by source network and those of verified callers by issuer and subject, so neither crowds out the other; past a caller's limit its refusals become one summary record with the count and the EHR ids they named, and past a budget one summary record per issuer and outcome is written. Security-event refusals are recorded for every answered request, and a refusal record the system log cannot take is counted in `atna_audit_refusals_unwritten`. The limits, windows and budgets are kept per replica.
