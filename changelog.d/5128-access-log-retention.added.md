- Each openEHR access-log entry states its own retention: `retain_until`, the last day it is kept, and the grounds of its period, the longest its categories and origins call for. The audit store's hourly retention worker replaces every entry past that day by its tombstone in place, which keeps the entry's uid, `system_id`, `time_recorded` and chain link, so the chain still verifies; a tombstone is never replaced again. An erasure a law orders takes the same path through `POST /ferroehr/rest/extensions/v1/admin/access-log/erase`, with the law's provisions as the tombstone's grounds, and is recorded as an access (#5128).
