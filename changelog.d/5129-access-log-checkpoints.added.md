- The access log's records can be sealed in signed checkpoints and verified against them, and one EHR's log moves with the EHR. `[audit.access_log]` turns on scheduled checkpoints (`checkpoints`, `checkpoint_interval`), a signature on every link (`sign_links`) and the certificates of the systems whose exported logs are imported (`trusted_key_files`); checkpoints and signed links use the `[record.signing]` OpenPGP key and refuse to start without it. `ferroehr access-log checkpoint`, `verify`, `export` and `import`, and `POST /admin/access-log/checkpoint`, `POST /admin/access-log/verify` and `GET /admin/access-log/export`, seal, verify and export; an import verifies the export against its checkpoint, keeps the records' `system_id`, links and `retain_until`, and records itself as an entry.
