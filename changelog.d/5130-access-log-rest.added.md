- The openEHR access log can be read over ITS-REST. `GET /ehr/{ehr_id}/access_log` and `GET /ehr/{ehr_id}/access_log/{uid}` under the extensions root serve the subject view to the person the EHR is about and to their bound representative (EHDS Art. 9(2)), leaving out the credential, the channel, the query digest, the labels and the link, and the full records to a reviewer the admin class admits; any other EHR answers `404` to the person, and the person's own reads of the log appear only with `include_self=true`. `GET /admin/access-log`, `GET /admin/access-log/chain` and `GET /admin/access-log/checkpoint` serve the review across EHRs, the links of a span of the chain and a stored checkpoint. Every read writes its own access-log entry.
