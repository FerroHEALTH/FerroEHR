- A `403` for a caller kind is recorded with its own binding code: `refused-system-personal-scope` for a system caller holding a `user/` or `patient/` scope, `refused-no-agreement` for an external caller whose issuer has no agreement for its accessor kind, and `refused-scope-beyond-agreement` for an external caller holding a scope its agreement does not read. Audit migration `0024` widens the `binding` constraint; the problem text of each refusal is unchanged.
