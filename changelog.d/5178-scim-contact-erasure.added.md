- A deleted SCIM User's contacts are erased from every version of its `PERSON`, keeping the name, the binding key and the identifiers for the record-keeping duties that need them. `[scim] contact_erasure_minutes` (default `0`, at most 43200) sets when: `0` erases them in the `DELETE` itself, a period leaves them to a sweep that runs every minute. Each erased version's node rows are derived again from the new body, its server signature is removed, and the erasure is an access record naming `party:<id>#contacts`. Party migration 0030 adds the `scim_contact_erasure` table.
