- `openehr-base` gives identifier and time parts as written: `ObjectVersionId::object_id_str` and `ObjectVersionId::versioned_object_id` keep the case of the `object_id` part, `Iso8601Time::fraction_digits` and `Iso8601DateTime::fraction_digits` return the digits of the fractional second as text, and `Iso8601Duration::parts` returns each component's designator and number verbatim (`DurationDesignator`, `DurationPart`), with `Iso8601Duration::fraction_digits` for the seconds.
