- A party commit no longer re-derives the login binding's side table, the reverse-relationship index and the protection check from the whole stored body. Each hook reads what the committed body holds, derived once in memory, and writes only the rows that changed, and `ferroehr party-binding refill` derives the rows with the same code. A party commit without a server signature no longer reassembles its body. A membership change at the cap of a 5 000-member SCIM Group went from 810 ms to 358 ms at p99 on a developer laptop.
