- The AOM 1.4 model follows schema revision 1.4.0.3 of the openEHR AM specification. `C_QUANTITY_ITEM` gains the optional `units_system` and `units_display_name` in canonical JSON (SPECAM-109), and `ARCHETYPE.uid` is the `UUID` inherited from `AUTHORED_RESOURCE` (SPECPR-515). An ADL 1.4 header `uid` that is not a UUID, such as an ISO OID, never fills the archetype's `uid` and stays verbatim in `other_meta_data`. In `openehr-am`, `v1_4::Archetype::uid` changes type from `HierObjectId` to `Uuid`, a breaking change.
