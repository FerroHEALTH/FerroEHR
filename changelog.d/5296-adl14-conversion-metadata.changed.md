- The ADL 1.4 to ADL 2 conversion follows the meta-data rules of the AOM 2 specification (`AOM2/master01-preface.adoc`, SPECAM-107). Each line of a translation's `other_contributors` detail becomes one entry of that translation's `other_contributors`. The original language's copyright becomes the archetype's copyright, a translation's differing copyright stays with that translation, and an identical one is dropped. The converted archetype's `conversion_details` records `source_model`, `tool` and `time`. Before, the conversion took the copyright of the alphabetically first language and dropped an ADL 1.4 description item's `copyright` attribute. In `openehr-adl`, `convert_opt_to_adl2` takes the conversion time, and `ConvertConfig` gains `source_model`, `tool` and `time`.
