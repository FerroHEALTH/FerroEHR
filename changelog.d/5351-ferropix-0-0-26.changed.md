- The compose quickstart, the hosted sandbox and the Helm chart deploy
  FerroPIX 0.0.26
  (`ghcr.io/ferrohealth/ferropix:0.0.26@sha256:85d40e6fdcdb39f782007eff91eb059861a4e039ca3f8fb56e37f2fa0f28d78a`,
  the FerroPIX chart 0.3.19) and FerroTERM 0.4.5
  (`ghcr.io/ferrohealth/ferroterm:0.4.5@sha256:7c20d39e1989fce1dfb92e6ddfd853dfa9a52bb9b7b973fbba42430963ef75bd`),
  and the development issuer is
  `mock-oauth2-server` 6.1.5. FerroPIX's configuration moves to the
  `[[auth.issuer]]` shape it now requires: the issuer is `kind = "machines"`
  and carries its own `audience`, and `[auth] audience` is gone. A chart
  deployment that sets `ferropix.config.auth` moves its audience onto each
  issuer the same way.
