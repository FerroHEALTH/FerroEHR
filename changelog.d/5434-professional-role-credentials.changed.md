- The default professional definitions follow openEHR CKM more closely: the professional `ROLE` (`openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-ROLE.healthcare_professional.v1.0.0`) records a clinician's registrations and qualifications as its `CAPABILITY`, adapted from the CKM `individual_credentials` archetype, and the professional `PERSON` names its name by usage and its email address by type and usage, as CKM codes them, with free text still accepted. Every default definition's `derived_from` names its CKM citeable id and modification time, and the representation relationship is re-cited to the person-to-person relationship of the CKM `PERSON` archetypes. The text of each definition changes under the same id, so a definition store that holds the 5.7.0 text of the professional `PERSON` or `ROLE` refuses `ferroehr definitions load` of the new one with `409`.
