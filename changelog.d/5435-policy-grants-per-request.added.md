- Every authenticated request resolves its caller's policy grants from the party store, read from coded content in parties of any archetype: a capability grants an operation when its credentials hold a `DV_CODED_TEXT` of the `ferroehr-operations` terminology (`commit_composition`, `import_ehr`, `import_ehr_extract`, `export_ehr_extract`, `federated_person_access`, `federated_organisation_access`, `read_subject_audit`, `access_service`, `read_identifiers`), and a client is the party carrying a `DV_IDENTIFIER` of type `client_id` under the token issuer. A system client resolves by its token's issuer and `client_id`, a bound person by their `PERSON`, each to the operations of the `ROLE`s it performs that hold on the request's date by the `ROLE`'s and the capability's `time_validity` and at its instant by the `not_before` and `not_after` elements. A grant changed through the Demographic API decides the caller's next request on every replica with no restart, a second party naming the same client is refused with `409`, and a request whose grants cannot be read is `503`. Each replica keeps the grants in memory and reads them again when a commit notifies it, so a request adds no database read. `ferroehr party-binding refill` rebuilds them. See the book's "Operational policy" page for the terminology contract.
- `[authz.grants] allowed` (`FERROEHR__AUTHZ__GRANTS__ALLOWED`), the operations the party store may grant. A `ROLE` committed with a capability coding an operation it does not list is refused with `403`, and a stored one is never honoured. The default, `["commit_composition"]`, admits system client writes only; an import, an export, a federation agreement and each privilege are admitted by name.
