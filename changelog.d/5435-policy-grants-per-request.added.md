- Every authenticated request resolves its caller's policy grants from the party store: a system client by its token's issuer and `client_id` to the system `AGENT` carrying that client identifier, a bound person by their `PERSON`, each to the capabilities of the policy `ROLE`s it performs that hold on the request's date by the `ROLE`'s and the capability's `time_validity` and at its instant by the credentials' `Not before` and `Not after`. A grant changed through the Demographic API decides the caller's next request on every replica with no restart, a second `AGENT` naming the same client is refused with `409`, and a request whose grants cannot be read is `503`. Each replica keeps the grants in memory and reads them again when a commit notifies it, so a request adds no database read. `ferroehr party-binding refill` rebuilds them, and with no party mapping configured rebuilds them alone. No check decides a request on a grant yet; a check reads them with `Capabilities::current()`. See the book's "Operational policy" page.
- `[authz.grants] allowed` (`FERROEHR__AUTHZ__GRANTS__ALLOWED`), the kinds of grant the party store may hold. A `ROLE` committed with a capability of a kind it does not list is refused with `403`, and a stored one is never honoured. The default, `["system_client.relay", "system_client.device"]`, admits the system client grants only; a migration, a federation agreement and each privilege are admitted by name.
