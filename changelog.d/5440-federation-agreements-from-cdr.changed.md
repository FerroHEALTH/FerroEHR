- Federation agreements are read from the CDR on every request. An agreement is a grant of `federated_person_access` or `federated_organisation_access` on the partner's party, which carries a `client_id` identifier for each client its `federation` issuer issues tokens to; its terms are elements of the capability's credentials named by `ferroehr-operations` codes (`agreement_name`, `agreement_version`, `jurisdiction`, `exchange`, `purpose`, `gdpr_art_6_1`, `gdpr_art_9_2`, `national_provision`, `consent`, `read_scope`, `template`, `discard_window`, `ehdsi_service`, `requests_per_second`, `burst`, `opt_out_override`, `access_class`, `requester_log_retention`), and its validity is the grant's own. A verified external caller is admitted only under the agreement its partner holds for its accessor kind, so a new, changed or ended agreement takes effect on the next request with no restart. Every legal check the configuration ran at boot now refuses the `ROLE` at its commit with `422`, naming each rule it breaks. `/management/info` and the boot line list every agreement in force with its partner's issuer and client. The legal checks hold no rule of one country: the jurisdiction is the agreement's own element, and the consent source is `registry` or `local`. See the book's "Operational policy" page, "Federation agreements".
