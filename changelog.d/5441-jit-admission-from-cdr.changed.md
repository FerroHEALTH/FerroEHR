- Just-in-time creation of a provisional `PERSON` at a first login is admitted by CDR data instead of configuration. An administrator writes a `ROLE` whose capability grants `ferroehr-operations::create_at_login` through the Demographic API; its credentials name the token issuer (`login_issuer`) and the admission conditions (`admit_idp`, `admit_group`, `admit_role`, `admit_assurance`, and `admit_guests` for a guest), and any grant in force admits a login that meets every condition it declares. Each first login reads the grants from the replica's in-memory copy, so a change applies to the next login with no restart. A grant that names no issuer or declares no condition is refused with `422`, and `[authz.grants] allowed` must list `create_at_login`, which the default does not. See the book's "Operational policy" page, "Just-in-time admission".
