- **Breaking: a party's inline relationship must be sourced at that party on every write.** RM demographic `PARTY.Relationships_validity` (`relationships.for_all (r | r.source = self)`) was judged only on a `PUT` whose body carried no `uid`. A `POST /demographic/{kind}`, a demographic CONTRIBUTION version, and a `PUT` whose body `uid` named another party's container committed a relationship sourced at another party, which then showed in that party's `reverse_relationships`. Each is now refused `422` naming `PARTY.Relationships_validity` and the relationship's `source`, and commits nothing. A new party's container id is assigned by the commit, so a create that carries a relationship is refused: add the relationship with an update once the party exists. A `PUT`, or a CONTRIBUTION version that modifies a party, whose body `uid` names another container than the one it writes is refused `422` at `uid`.
