- A party read of an older version, by its `OBJECT_VERSION_ID` or at a `version_at_time`, listed in `reverse_relationships` the relationships other parties hold today. It now lists them as they stood when that version was committed, and so do a `VERSION<PARTY>` read and the representation a create or update returns, which carried no `reverse_relationships` before. The reverse-relationship index keeps when each relationship held: the party migration `0033_relationship_target_validity` rebuilds it from the stored party versions, once, at startup.
