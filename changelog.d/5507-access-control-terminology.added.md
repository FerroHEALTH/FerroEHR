- The `ferroehr-operations` terminology gains the access control groups the
  access evaluator and the access write checks read: `access default`,
  `access directive kind`, `access effect`, `access relationship`, `access
  caller kind`, `access operation`, `access sensitivity clearance`, `access
  permitted purpose`, `care relationship` and `access refusal`, documented in
  the book's operational policy page as a stable contract. A code outside its
  group, or a value of another terminology, is refused. Two grants join the
  operation codes, and `[authz.grants] allowed` accepts them:
  `write_care_relationship` and `write_access_policy`.
