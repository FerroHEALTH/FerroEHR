- The EHDS Art. 8 restriction of access by health professionals and the Art. 10 opt-out from the access services are `restriction` and `opt_out` directives of the EHR's own `EHR_ACCESS` settings, so they are versioned with it and travel in an EHR Extract. The controller commits them on the person's instruction with `POST /admin/ehr-access` (`{"ehr_id", "preceding_version_uid", "settings"}`, `412` when the version moved on) and reads every version with `GET /admin/ehr-access?ehr_id=…`. Restricted and opted-out data still reads as absent (`404`), and the emergency overrides still mark the access. A `restriction` or `opt_out` directive with `emergency_override` false is refused `400`, and so is a restricted object id that names no versioned object.
