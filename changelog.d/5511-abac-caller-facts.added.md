- **Caller facts for the access evaluator.** A `CAPABILITY` whose credentials hold an element named `ferroehr-operations::sensitivity_clearance` or `permitted_purpose`, valued with a code of the jurisdiction's terminology, clears that sensitivity or permits that purpose of use. A `PERSON`'s `PARTY_RELATIONSHIP` carrying `member_of` or `under_care_of` is a care relationship, in any archetype. Every replica holds these facts beside the grants. An admission or a discharge decides the next request through one indexed read of the parties that changed. Party migration `0039` adds `binding_caller_fact` and `binding_caller_fact_party`.
- **The `write_care_relationship` grant.** Only a caller holding it, such as a hospital's ADT system, or an administrator commits a `PERSON` version that changes a care relationship; anyone else gets `403`. A malformed clearance or purpose, a care relationship that is not a `PERSON`'s, and a standalone care relationship are refused `422`.
- **Default definitions** `openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-PARTY_RELATIONSHIP.member_of.v1.0.0` and `openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-PARTY_RELATIONSHIP.under_care_of.v1.0.0`, carried by the binary and attached to each release.
