- The controller's access policy is a versioned `ACCESS_POLICY` stored in the CDR: which kinds of data carry which sensitivity, whether clinical access needs a care relationship and a declared purpose, and the catalogue of named directives a patient accepts by reference. `GET`, `PUT` with `If-Match` (or `If-None-Match: *` for the first version) and the version history are served under `{ext}/admin/access_policy`; every version is committed in its own contribution with its audit and checked strictly against the draft access control model. The default archetype `openEHR-EHR-ACCESS_POLICY.controller.v1` is stored at boot, and each replica holds the policy in memory, read again on the next request after a write on any replica.
