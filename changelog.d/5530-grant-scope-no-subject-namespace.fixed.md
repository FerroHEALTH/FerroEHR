- An import or export grant can reach an EHR whose `EHR_STATUS.subject` has no `external_ref`, such as an anonymous record's `PARTY_SELF`, which no grant could reach once `[authz.rbac] import_role` was removed: a `subject_namespace` element holding the `DV_CODED_TEXT` `no_subject_namespace` of `ferroehr-operations` covers exactly those EHRs, for `import_ehr`, `import_ehr_extract` and `export_ehr_extract`. A grant naming real namespaces still does not reach them, the code reaches no EHR whose subject names a namespace, and a grant naming no `subject_namespace` is still refused at its commit. See the book's "Operational policy" page.
