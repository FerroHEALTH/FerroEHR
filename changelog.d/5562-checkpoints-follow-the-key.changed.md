- The access log is sealed whenever `[record.signing]` holds an OpenPGP key: the server writes a signed checkpoint every five minutes when the chain grew, and seals nothing without a key. `[audit.access_log] checkpoints`, `checkpoint_interval` and `sign_links` are gone and refused as unknown keys. A local link is no longer signed on its own (migration `audit/0029` drops `audit.access_log_record.signature`); a signed link in another system's export still verifies on import.
