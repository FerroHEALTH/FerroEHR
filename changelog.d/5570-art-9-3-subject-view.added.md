- The controller's access policy can declare the restrictions a Member State law lays on the access log's subject view under EHDS Art. 9(3). Each entry of the new `ACCESS_POLICY.log_restrictions` (an `ACCESS_LOG_RESTRICTION` of the draft access control package) names the accessor kinds it applies to, the accessor members withheld (`accessor_party`, `accessor_provider`, `accessor_roles` or `accessor_groups`, the new `access log withheld` group of `ferroehr-operations`) and its legal grounds. `GET /ehr/{ehr_id}/access_log` and its `/{uid}` serve the subject and their representative the entry without those members, a reviewer still reads it whole, and the stored entry never changes. A policy write applies on the next request with no restart, and a write that changes the restrictions writes an access-log entry of its own. The default archetype `openEHR-EHR-ACCESS_POLICY.controller.v1.0.0` constrains the restrictions. FerroEHR ships no country's restriction.
