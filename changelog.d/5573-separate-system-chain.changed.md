- The access log is now a hash chain of its own, apart from the records outside it (an authentication, a template, party or application operation), which keep theirs in `audit.audit_event`. `GET /admin/access-log/export` and `ferroehr access-log export` no longer refuse an EHR's log because such a record was written between its entries, and the export and the chain read no longer answer `422` or `400` for it. Audit migration 0031 splits the shared chain once: the access log is renumbered from 1 and linked again from its own genesis, the link signatures and checkpoints of the shared chain are removed (the next scheduled checkpoint seals the new head), and the positions the access log held become recorded gaps of the system chain, so both chains verify. `SELECT * FROM audit.verify_access_log_chain()` verifies the access log, `audit.verify_audit_chain()` the system chain, and the scheduled chain check runs both. The system chain is sealed with the same key on the same schedule, and `ferroehr audit-chain seal` and `ferroehr audit-chain verify [--seal <file>]` seal and verify it on demand. The erasure of an entry (`POST /admin/access-log/erase`) is stored once, as its access-log entry, which the ITI-81 search and the FHIR feed now serve; no `audit_event` row is written for it. `audit.audit_record`, `audit.access_trail` and `audit.fhir_feed_outbox` name each record by its `chain` and position.
