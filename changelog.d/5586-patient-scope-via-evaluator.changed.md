- The access evaluator alone confines a patient to their own EHR. A caller
  with a SMART App Launch patient-context credential is the `subject` caller
  kind on the EHR its launch context names and is refused every other EHR
  with `403` `refused-consent-required`, whether the operation names that
  EHR in its path, its body or its result, DIRECTORY operations included; an
  AQL query reads only the patient's own rows, and its counts and pages see
  only those. A patient-context token whose launch context names no EHR
  reaches none. The provider of the caller's party binding answers EHDS
  Annex II 3.2(a) in the BALP `AuditEvent` of every access record.
