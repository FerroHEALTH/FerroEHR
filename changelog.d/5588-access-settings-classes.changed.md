- Each replica no longer holds every EHR that carries `EHR_ACCESS` settings. The projection now stores each EHR's settings as a settings class (what they share with other EHRs) and its facts (instants, parties, objects and origins). A query reads each EHR's class and facts in its own SQL, so its counts and pages stay exact. A replica holds at most 4096 EHRs' settings and the classes, about 40 MB, wherever 1 000 000 EHRs carry settings (10.1 GB before). An AQL request builds its access condition in 8 µs at that scale (880 ms before), and it no longer sends every such EHR id to the database. An EHR of a class a replica has not copied yet contributes no row until the replica copies it. Migration `0042` replaces the unread `access_directive` and `access_label` tables with `access_class`, and the next boot refills the projection.
