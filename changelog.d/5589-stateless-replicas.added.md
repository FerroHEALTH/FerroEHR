- A restriction, an opt-out or a withdrawn consent committed on one instance is never admitted on another before that instance's notification arrives. Every read that leaves the store compares the revision of the EHR's `EHR_ACCESS` settings row it reads with the one its verdict was judged on and refuses on a mismatch, and every commit judges the EHR again from its own transaction. No request adds a statement.
- The policy listeners hold connections of their own, apart from the request pools, so the pools may run through PgBouncer in transaction mode. The new keys `[database] listen_url`, `[database.clinical] listen_url` and `[database.party] listen_url` (each with a `listen_url_file` form) point them at the primary or a session-mode pooler; unset, they connect on the database URL.
- An `instances` health indicator reads `DEGRADED` when another running instance of the deployment signs with another key or runs another configuration, naming it. Each instance compares itself with the others at start and every 30 seconds through the new `clinical.instance` table.
- `docker-compose.replicas.yml` runs a second instance beside the quickstart, and the deployment probe runs the two-instance probes against it.
