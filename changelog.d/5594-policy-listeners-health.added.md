- A `policy_listeners` health indicator reads `DEGRADED` while the `LISTEN` connection of any of the four policy copies is down (`ehr_access`, `grants`, `policy_registers`, `access_policy`), and names each lost one. While a listener is down, its copy reads the database on every request, so the instance stays in rotation. The new book page "Running several instances" covers running instances behind a load balancer on one PostgreSQL primary: why the listeners need a direct or session-mode connection, the connection budget, and the shared signing key and configuration.
