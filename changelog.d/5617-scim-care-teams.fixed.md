- A client holding `write_care_relationship`, such as a hospital's ADT system, now rosters a SCIM User: a version of the User's `PERSON` whose only change is to its membership (`MBR`) and `under_care_of` relationships passes the party protection, and any other change stays `403`.
- A SCIM client can now remove a member from a Group, or add one, while the member's `PERSON` holds a care relationship the ADT system wrote. The care check compares what each care relationship says and no longer its position in `relationships` (#5640).
