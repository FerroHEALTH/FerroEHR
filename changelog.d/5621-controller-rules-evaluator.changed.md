- **Controller rules decide as step 4 of the access evaluator.** A deny rule of the access policy that applies refuses with `403 refused-policy-denied`, whatever the patient's directives permit. It binds every caller but the patient on their own record, representatives included, and an emergency lifts it only where its `emergency_override` holds. A permit rule clears callers for the sensitivities it names, now step 5. The care relationship, purpose and default steps are now 6, 7 and 8, and the access record and the `access_emergency_overrides_total` label `rule` follow the new numbering.
- **Roles come from the CDR.** An actor scope's `role` matches the coded `name` of a `ROLE` in force that the caller performs, compared by terminology and code. A token's role claims decide only the RBAC class gate and drive no rule. Party migration `0041` holds the roles as caller facts. Run `ferroehr party-binding refill` once after upgrading so the `ROLE`s already stored are read.
- **A write is judged by the version it commits.** Steps 3 to 8 judge the template, root archetype, origins and classified sensitivities of a written version, for every caller, the external persons of a federation agreement included. A restriction and an opt-out still decide a write only for the EHR as a whole, so no write reveals one.
