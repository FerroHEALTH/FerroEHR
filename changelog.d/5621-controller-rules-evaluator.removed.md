- **Capability clearances and permitted purposes.** The `ferroehr-operations` codes `sensitivity_clearance` and `permitted_purpose`, their terminology groups, and their caller facts are gone. A capability element named by either is an unknown code, refused at commit. Write a permit rule in the access policy instead: scope it by role or party and name the sensitivities, the purposes, or both. Party migration `0042` drops the stored rows.
- **`refused-purpose-not-permitted`.** A purpose limit is a deny rule with a permit exception naming the purposes, refused `refused-policy-denied`. Step 7 refuses only a missing purpose.
- **The `template` element of a `commit_composition` grant.** It no longer limits what a system client commits. Write a deny rule whose `applies_to` names the client's `AGENT`, with a permit exception listing its templates (#5624). A federation agreement's `template` elements are unchanged.
