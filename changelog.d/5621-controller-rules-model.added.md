- **Controller rules in the access policy.** `ACCESS_POLICY` gains `rules`: the controller's own rules for every EHR it keeps, each an `ACCESS_DIRECTIVE` of the new kind `openehr_access::controller_rule` (EHDS Art. 11(3)). A policy whose rule has another kind, an EHR's `EHR_ACCESS` or a catalogue entry holding a controller rule, and a rule naming a role, purpose, sensitivity, category or legal ground in `openehr_access` are refused `400`. The default definition `openEHR-EHR-ACCESS_POLICY.controller.v1.0.0` admits the rules. The draft access control specification records the class, its invariants and its mappings to FHIR R5 `Permission` and IHE APPC foundational policies.
