- A patient-portal login that a `PERSON` carries is now held to that `PERSON`: it is the patient only when that `PERSON` is the EHR's subject, and a representative only while it holds a live representation of the subject. Otherwise it is refused with `refused-representation-unrecorded`, decided at binding. This includes a representative with no representation yet and a representative of another patient only, who was previously admitted as the patient of the EHR the portal named. A login no `PERSON` carries is still the patient.
