- A patient portal's SCIM client now provisions the portal's representatives. A `scim_provision` grant may name a `patients` issuer as its `binding_issuer`, and that client's Users are `PERSON`s of the patient template with no professional `ROLE`. Their binding key is the portal login. The default party mapping lists the patient template as a second, non-default person template. SCIM writes the identity only: any version from a SCIM client that adds, changes or drops a representation is refused `403`, because the representation is the patient's or an administrator's to write (EHDS Art. 4(2)).
