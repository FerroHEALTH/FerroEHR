- **A pseudonymous EHR reaches its patient's care relationships and representatives.** A patient's `PERSON` declares the EHR subject reference it stands for, such as the pseudonym `[pixm]` stores in `EHR_STATUS.subject`, by an element of its `details` named by the `record_subject` code of FerroEHR's operation terminology, whose `DV_IDENTIFIER` holds the namespace as `issuer` and the pseudonym as `id`. The access evaluator resolves an EHR's subject to that `PERSON` from each replica's bounded care copy, with no database transaction on a hit, one indexed read on a miss and no call to the identity source, so `care_relationship_required` and representations work under `record.subject_index = "pixm"` as under `"local"`. One reference stands for one `PERSON` (`409` otherwise); an identity merge moves a pseudonym to the survivor's `PERSON` in one demographic contribution, and a representative of a merged `PERSON` reaches each of its EHRs. Writing the element needs the `write_care_relationship` grant or an administrator; an element that is no `DV_IDENTIFIER` with an issuer and an id, sits outside a `PERSON`'s `details`, names the CDR's own `[authz.party_binding] namespace`, or names a `[privacy] subject_namespaces` namespace with an id the subject rule refuses is `422`. The patient archetype `openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-PERSON.patient.v1.0.0` carries the element (`id36`). The party domain now links a pseudonym to its `PERSON` for every patient whose `PERSON` carries the element, a trade-off the book's pseudonymisation boundary names; the clinical domain still holds the pseudonym alone. Migrations `party/0043` and `clinical/0046`.
