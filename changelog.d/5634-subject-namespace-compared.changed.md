- **An EHR subject names a `PERSON` of the party store only under the CDR's own namespace.** The access evaluator compares `EHR_STATUS.subject.external_ref.namespace` with `[authz.party_binding] namespace` before it reads the id as a `PERSON` container; a container id under any other namespace names a `PERSON` only through a `record_subject` element, and care relationships and representations fail closed on it otherwise.
