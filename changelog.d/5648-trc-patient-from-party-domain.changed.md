- A MyHealth@EU contact point's TRC patient (`ehdsi.patient.id`, `system|value`) is now resolved in the party domain, and its requests no longer call the PIX Manager. The patient is the `PERSON` that carries the identifier at the `[authz.party_binding.patient] identifier` path, matched on the issuer and the value (a protected-scheme value through its lookup digest), and the request may name any EHR that `PERSON`'s own reference or its `record_subject` references name. Under `record.subject_index = "local"` the EHR whose `EHR_STATUS` names the identifier is found too. An identifier no `PERSON`, or more than one, carries answers `trc-patient-mismatch`. Migration `party/0044` adds the projection `binding_patient_identifier`, and a party refill fills it. Upgrade step: under `pixm`, the registering system writes the patient identifiers a contact point names into the patient's `PERSON`, in the same contribution as its `record_subject` references.
