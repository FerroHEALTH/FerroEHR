- **The access codes are vendor-neutral.** The terminology the operation grants, their qualifiers, the access control groups and the access log groups are coded in is `openehr_access`, the draft "openEHR access (draft)" terminology the access control change request proposes to openEHR; the former terminology, named after FerroEHR, is gone and no longer read. Where an HL7 code system publishes a code of the same meaning, the record carries that code under the code system's id: a directive's effect is `consent-provision-type` `permit` or `deny`, the subject caller kind and the subject-of-care accessor kind are `extra-security-role-type` `datasubject`, a care-team membership is `v3-RoleClass` `MBR` (formerly `member_of`), and the access log's read, create, update and delete actions are `audit-event-action` `R`, `C`, `U` and `D`. The access log's outcome, accessor kind and authorisation codes move from `openehr` to `openehr_access`, and its refusal reasons `opted out` and `restricted by the subject` give way to the `refused-opt-out` and `refused-restricted` codes. The draft terminology page lists the HL7 code systems with their canonical URIs, the new `representation` group and the `TERM_MAPPING`s of the codes that stay. The access log's `accessor_kind` filter matches the kind's rubric. The shipped definitions that name the codes change in place at their versions, with their digests in `published.sha256`. Party migration `0043` keys a membership's caller fact by `MBR` and marks the policy parties by the new codes; a party version committed with the former codes stays as committed and grants nothing.
