- The access log's retention is the controller's access policy: `ACCESS_POLICY.log_retention` states whether Regulation (EU) 2025/327 binds the controller, the period each entry is kept from the date of access (whole years or days; absent keeps it without end) and the floors and ceilings the controller's law sets, each with its provision. A policy write whose period breaks a declared bound, or the three years of EHDS Art. 9(2) in the Union, is refused with `400`; a new version applies to every record written after it, on every replica. FerroEHR ships no table of national law. `ACCESS_RETENTION.grounds` names EHDS Art. 9(2), coded by the regulation's European Legislation Identifier (`http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj`, `art_9.par_2`), then each declared bound, and the new `ACCESS_RETENTION.policy` names the policy version. The `ferroehr-access-log-retention` terminology, its `store-horizon` code and the `ehds-logging` ground codes leave the entry. A new `log_retention` readiness indicator reads `degraded` while the access policy states no retention. The draft access control BMM gains `ACCESS_LOG_RETENTION` and `ACCESS_RETENTION_BOUND`, and the default `ACCESS_POLICY` archetype constrains `log_retention`, both changed in place.
