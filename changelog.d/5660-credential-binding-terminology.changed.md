- The access log codes `accessor.binding` in the new `credential binding` group of the draft `openehr_access` terminology, in place of FerroEHR's own `ferroehr-party-binding`. The group holds one code per party binding outcome without a `refused-` prefix (`bound`, `unbound`, `party inactive`, `no agreement` and the rest), and the draft access log BMM adds the `ACCESSOR.Binding_valid` invariant, which the store checks on every entry it reads back.
