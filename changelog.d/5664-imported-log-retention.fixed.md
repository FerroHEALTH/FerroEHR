- An access-log entry imported with an EHR from another system is now replaced by its tombstone once the `retain_until` it states has passed, as a local entry is; before, it was kept with its content for ever. Its tombstone is kept as long again, and then the hourly run folds, per exporting system and within each span between two imported checkpoints, the positions before the first imported record still kept (tombstones past their end and the other EHRs' links) into `audit.access_log_imported_gap` (migration `audit/0034`), so every kept imported record stays hash-chained to its checkpoint with or without link signatures. `ferroehr access-log verify` and `POST /admin/access-log/verify` now also verify every imported log against each of its checkpoints and report what fails under `imported`. An import refuses a link or gap that ends where a stored one ends with another digest, and does not bring back a tombstone or expired entry a gap already holds. The metrics `access_log_tombstones_total`, `access_log_folded_total` and `access_log_gaps_total` carry `chain` = `local` or `imported`. No configuration key was added or removed.
