- A key issuer of the party binding (a `patients` issuer, or the `identifier_issuer` of an `[[auth.party]]` table) is added and removed while the replicas serve, with no replica stopped and no `ferroehr party-binding refill`. Each key issuer is a key space of its own. A replica that names a new one records it `pending`. Its logins answer `503` with a `Retry-After` until every live replica names it, and then the replicas fill its keys from the party versions and it turns `active`. A replica that drops an issuer turns it `retiring`: its logins are refused with `403 refused-unbound` on every replica at once, and its keys are deleted once no live replica names it. Changing whether an issuer is a `patients` issuer runs as a removal followed by an addition. A change to the templates, selectors, role, group or membership of `[authz.party_binding]` still refuses to start until `ferroehr party-binding refill` has run with every replica stopped. The stored mapping digest no longer covers the key issuers, so the first boot after the upgrade fills the side table once.
