- An access-log entry is stored in two parts: the part its caller shares (accessor, credential, roles, authorisations, purposes, retention and the caller's access details) once in `audit.access_log_shared`, keyed by its SHA-256, and the rest on its row, compressed in place (migration `audit/0038`). The entry stays one record with its own link: the server computes `content_digest` over the whole canonical form when it writes the entry and recomputes it from the joined parts when it verifies, and the SQL verifier checks the links, the gaps, the head and the search columns. An access detail the entry's core already states is no longer repeated in `other_details` (the principal, issuer, client and token the credential names, the roles and purpose the accessor lists, the EHR, the action and outcome the core codes, and the delivered count), and the details are written in the order of their codes. A tombstone keeps no shared part, and a shared part leaves with the last row that named it. `audit.access_trail` reads each column from the detail and else from the core. Measured on a local rig over 2 105 entries, storage per entry fell from 3 362 to 2 078 bytes. No configuration key was added or removed.
