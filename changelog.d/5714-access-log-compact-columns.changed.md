- The access log stores its link digests and the shared part's key as 32-byte `bytea` and an entry's uid as a `uuid`, and drops its `system_id` and `request_id` columns, which the entry's content already holds (migration `audit/0039`). Every function, view and route that hands a digest on still writes it in lower-case hexadecimal, so a link reads as before. What repeats of an entry's `outcome` and `query` joins the shared part, the row keeping the delivered count and the rows. An entry no longer repeats the object its accessed item names in an `other_details` element; the reader and `audit.access_trail` take it from the item. The EHR index also carries the chain position, so a person's entries are read newest first without a sort. Measured on a local rig over 2 105 entries, storage per entry fell from 2 015 to 1 938 bytes; a row under PostgreSQL's 2 kB TOAST threshold is stored uncompressed, so the narrower columns pay off only with the smaller row the finer split gives.
