- A capacity test, `scripts/perf/capacity.sh` and the dispatch-only workflow `perf-capacity.yml`, steps an open-loop arrival rate up per request group and for a stated mix until the knee: the highest rate with no failed request, every p99 within its SLO and throughput equal to offered load. It runs one instance or two behind a load balancer on one PostgreSQL 18, over the perf gate's access seed with the access check and the signed access log on, and records server, PostgreSQL and generator CPU, `pg_stat_statements`, backend waits, the pool and the audit drain at every step. The book's Capacity page explains how to run it and read the result.
