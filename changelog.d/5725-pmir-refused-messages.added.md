- An administrator can list, retry and settle the ITI-93 messages the PMIR consumer refused, through `GET /admin/pmir/messages`, `POST /admin/pmir/messages/{message_id}/retry` and `POST /admin/pmir/messages/{message_id}/settle` (the Admin class and `[auth] product_scope`) or `ferroehr pmir list|retry|settle --operator <name>`. The list names each message's refusal code, receipt and refusal time, apply attempts, the Registry identities it names and the `PERSON`s it keeps split, with no identifier value. A retry applies the message again at its arrival position once its cause is fixed, and is refused once a later message about the same identity has applied. A settlement closes it unapplied with a recorded decision and reason: `discard` keeps its splits, `lift-splits` removes them. Every list, retry and settlement is an access record naming the operator and `pmir-message:<id>`. The `pmir_messages_refused` gauge counts the refused messages, and the `pmir_refused_messages` health indicator reads `DEGRADED` from the new `[pix] refused_degraded_at` (default 1) on. Migration `party/0049` adds the message's identities, attempts, refusal time and settlement to `pmir_message`.
