- AQL compares and orders a whole data value by its own kind, never by its serialized JSON: a number by the magnitude or value of a whole `DV_QUANTITY`, `DV_COUNT`, `DV_PROPORTION`, `DV_ORDINAL`, `DV_SCALE` or `DV_DURATION`, an ISO 8601 date or date-time by the instant of a whole `DV_DATE` or `DV_DATE_TIME`, and a time by that of a whole `DV_TIME`; any other pair keeps no row. Before, `> '9999-12-31'` kept every `DV_DATE`, `= '2022-03-03'` kept none, and an `ORDER BY` sorted `400` before `42`.
