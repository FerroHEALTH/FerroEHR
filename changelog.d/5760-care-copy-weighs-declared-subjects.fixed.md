- The care relationship copy's bound now weighs the record subjects a team's patients declare, which it left out (#5760). A census used to count only its patients, so a team whose patients declare identity-source subjects could take four to five times more memory than the bound allowed. The bound is now 64 MiB of censuses, about 270 000 patients that each declare one subject. `access_copy_entries` adds `care_subjects`, `care_teams`, `care_patients`, `care_declared` and `caller_facts`, and the new `access_copy_size_bytes` reports the bytes the care teams take.
