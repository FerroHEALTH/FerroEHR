- The access log is sealed in a signed checkpoint at least once a second while entries are chained, and once when the chainer comes to rest, where every chainer round signed one. Under load a chained entry waits up to a second for the checkpoint that covers it; at rest it is covered at once, and an import or an erasure still seals its own entries. At 3000 requests a second this saves about 20 signatures and their waits a second.
