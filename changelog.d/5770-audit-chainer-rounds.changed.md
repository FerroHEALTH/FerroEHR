- The access-log chainer starts its rounds at most 50 ms apart, unless a round filled its batch of 512, where it ran back to back whenever a request had written since the last round: at 3000 entries a second it took about 10 entries a round, each with its own signed checkpoint and commit. Its read of the oldest intake rows uses the intake's primary key, where PostgreSQL scanned and sorted the whole table, hundreds of megabytes of deleted rows, while the round held the chain head.
