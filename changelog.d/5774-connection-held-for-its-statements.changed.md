- A request gives its party connection back to the pool when its clinical work begins, where it used to keep it idle until the handler answered, and a write keeps no connection while its body is validated and its large media are offloaded. On the reads, commits and single-EHR queries measured, a request now holds its party connection for 0.5 to 0.7 ms, against 1.3 to 1.6 ms for a read, 10 to 12 ms for a commit and 3.6 to 4.9 ms for a query before, so the party pool runs dry later under load. See [Sizing the pool](https://ferroehr.eu/docs/latest/installation/config-server.html#sizing-the-pool).
