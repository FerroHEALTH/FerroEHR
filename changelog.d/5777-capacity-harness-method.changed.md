- The capacity test separates FerroEHR's latency from the host it runs on. Every step reports the server's own p50, p95 and p99 per group from its request log beside the generator's, and passes only when both are within the SLO; the summary names the forwarder floor from a `health` step, and `CAPACITY_GENERATOR=network` runs the generator on the servers' network with no port forwarder between. `CAPACITY_REPEAT` runs a step several times and reports each median p99 with its spread, and `capacity.sh compare` calls a difference between two arms only outside both spreads. `capacity.sh snapshot` and `restore` start an arm, or every run under `CAPACITY_FRESH`, from the seeded store, and `capacity.sh arms` interleaves configurations on it. `CAPACITY_CONTINUE` runs a sweep past a failed step and records each step's outcome. Every step records the host's and the VM's load, the clock source and the CPU of every other container, the peak memory of each instance and of PostgreSQL, and the store's bytes per composition version and per access-log entry. The Capacity workflow runs each step three times on its x86 runner by default, the host the book's Capacity page names for latency targets.
