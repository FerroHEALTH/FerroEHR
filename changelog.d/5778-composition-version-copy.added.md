- A read of a COMPOSITION by its version id is served from the instance's copy of the versions it read, up to 64 MiB, and makes no database statement (#5778). Every such read is still judged by the access evaluator and recorded in the access log. A new version, a restriction or its lifting, an access delay and an erasure drop the object's versions on every instance through the new `ferroehr_object_version` notification (migration `clinical/0051`), and the copy is bypassed while its `object_versions` listener is down. An `If-None-Match` that names the version answers `304 Not Modified` with no body, recorded as the read it is. The copy's lookups and drops are counted as `object_version` in `access_copy_lookups_total` and `access_copy_drops_total`.
