- A read of a COMPOSITION by its version id takes the version's body from the instance's copy of the versions it read, up to 64 MiB (#5778). Before serving one, the instance confirms the object's state in one indexed statement: its head, its restriction and access delay marks, its EHR's settings revision and the version's content digest. A restriction, an erasure or a new version therefore takes effect at its commit on every instance. Every read is still judged by the access evaluator and recorded in the access log. An `If-None-Match` that names the version answers `304 Not Modified` with no body, recorded as the read it is. The copy is `object_version` in `access_copy_lookups_total`, `access_copy_drops_total`, `access_copy_entries` and `access_copy_size_bytes`.
