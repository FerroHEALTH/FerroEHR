- The scheduled verification of the audit chains catches up with any backlog. It walks the system chain and the access log from their cursors in steps of at most 20 000 records, one statement each, and every intact step moves the cursor, so a backlog that once outlasted the `[database] statement_timeout` and was reported `unverifiable` on every run is now verified and shows as a falling lag. Damage inside the backlog is still reported at its position. The new `audit_chain_verification_lag` and `audit_chain_unverified_age_seconds` gauges by `chain` give the lag, and the new `[audit.store] max_verify_lag` (default `"4h"`, `"0s"` for metrics only) makes the `audit_chain` indicator read `DEGRADED` when the oldest unverified record waited longer. The database functions `audit.verify_audit_chain_incremental()` and `audit.verify_access_log_chain_incremental()` are replaced by `audit.verify_audit_chain_step()` and `audit.verify_access_log_chain_step()` (audit migration 0041).
