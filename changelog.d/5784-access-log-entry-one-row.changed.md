- An access-log entry is stored in one row: its ITI-81 search columns, search tokens and render policy sit on the entry's own row in `audit.access_log_record`, and `audit.access_log_search` is a view of them. The row keeps no member a column already holds, the EHR id is a `uuid` and the render policy is named by its id, and every reader goes through `audit.access_log_document`. An entry takes less room on disk, and the search, the reads of an EHR's log, erasure, verification and every link digest answer as before.
