- FerroEHR runs behind FerroSMART, the family's SMART App Launch 2.x authorization server, from the quickstart: `docker-compose.ferrosmart.yml` adds FerroSMART (pinned by digest) with its own PostgreSQL, trusts it as the `persons` issuer with the `ferrosmart` profile, binds each person by FerroSMART's subject, honours its patient/ grants and describes it in the discovery document, and points the viewer's Launch button at it. `docker-compose.ferrosmart-keycloak.yml` or `docker-compose.ferrosmart-zitadel.yml` beside it runs Keycloak or ZITADEL upstream for login only, and `docker/ferrosmart/secrets.sh` makes the secrets. The hospital deployment scenario runs behind FerroSMART with every token FerroSMART's, once per upstream, in the new `deploy-probe-hospital` CI job; its patient launches go through FerroSMART's picker, its EHR launch through the viewer's Launch button, and the mock `patients` issuer is gone from it.
