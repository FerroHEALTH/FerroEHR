- An instance's connections to PostgreSQL now stay inside the new `[database] connection_budget` (default 40): its three pools and its listener connections together. Every notification channel of one database is held on one `LISTEN` connection, where each policy copy held one of its own (six in all), and the ITI-93 applier listens on it too instead of holding a connection of the party pool. An unset pool size is four per core, at least 10, within its even share of the budget (13 each at the default with one listener connection), so two instances at the defaults open at most 80 connections and fit PostgreSQL's default `max_connections` of 100; one instance previously opened up to 96. A `[database] max_connections` whose three pools and listener connections exceed the budget is refused at startup naming the budget, and the server warns when one instance alone may open more connections than its PostgreSQL admits. One-shot subcommands open only the connections they use. The `policy_listeners` health indicator and the `policy_listener_*` metrics now also name `registered_templates`. The Helm chart (38.0.0) sets `config.database.connection_budget: 30`, so its two replicas and the one a rollout adds open at most 90, in place of `config.database.max_connections: 10`. Upgrade: a deployment that set `max_connections` above 13 sets `connection_budget` to at least three times it plus one per listen DSN, inside PostgreSQL's `max_connections` over its instances.
