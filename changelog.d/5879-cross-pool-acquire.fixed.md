- A request that has to wait for a connection of one database first gives back the connections it keeps of the other databases. Before, two requests that took the clinical and the party database in opposite orders could each hold what the other waited for, and every request behind them waited until its client gave up, about ten seconds after a restart under load. A request that asks a database for a second connection while it holds one of it counts `db_pool_nested_acquires`, and a debug build refuses it.
