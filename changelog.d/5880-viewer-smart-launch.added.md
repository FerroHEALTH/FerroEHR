- The viewer launches a SMART app from an EHR's page through FerroSMART's EHR launch API. With the new `[launch]` section (`issuer`, `client_id`, `private_key_file`, `kid`, `resource`, `patient_namespaces`, `timeout`, `tls` and the `[[launch.app]]` list of `name`, `client_id` and `launch_url`), the EHR's More menu carries a Launch item per app. A click reads the EHR with your own session, registers `ehrId` (and `patient` for a subject in a configured namespace) at FerroSMART with the viewer's `private_key_jwt` client assertion, and opens the app in a new window at `{launch_url}?iss={resource}&launch={value}` with a fresh, single-use `launch` value. The key stays on the server and no launch value is logged. The pages' `form-action` names each app's origin beside `'self'`.
