- The access-log writes run fewer statements inside PostgreSQL (migration `audit/0043`). The digest functions of the search tokens, the search sets and the access-log links are inlined into the statements that call them, the access-log records and their search rows no longer carry foreign keys onto the shared parts, the render policies and the records (the chain move is their one writer, the render policies are append-only, and the verifier derives every record from its shared part again), and the link check of each chained record runs no statement of its own. Measured in process over the access evaluator with the local access log, a single-EHR AQL request went from 54 to 17 nested statements and a population AQL request serving five EHRs from 115 to 22. A test now holds each AQL request to a budget of round trips and nested statements, and the test servers load `pg_stat_statements` for it. No configuration key was added or removed.
