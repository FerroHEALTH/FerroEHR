- A write's audit records no longer wait for a second audit connection while they hold one. The first record written under a new render policy registered that policy on a connection of its own while the write held another, so after a start or a render-policy change a burst of concurrent writes could fill the audit pool and each wait for a connection none would give back, until the pool's acquire timeout. The store now registers the policy before the write takes its connection, in a transaction of its own, so a write that rolls back leaves it stored.
