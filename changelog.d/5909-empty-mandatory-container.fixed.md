- A composition whose template makes a container mandatory (existence 1..1) and lets it be empty (cardinality 0..*) commits with `"items": []`. The validator counted an empty list as a missing attribute and refused it `422` "mandatory attribute 'items' is missing", so no document could satisfy such a template and FerroEHR's own `medium` example of it was refused. A container left out entirely is still refused, and the cardinality check alone decides how many members it needs.
