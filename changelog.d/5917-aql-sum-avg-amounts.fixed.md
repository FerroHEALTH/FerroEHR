- `SUM` and `AVG` over a whole data value add only numbers and amounts (`DV_QUANTITY`, `DV_COUNT`, `DV_PROPORTION`, `DV_DURATION`). Over a `DV_DATE_TIME`, `DV_DATE`, `DV_TIME` or `DV_ORDINAL` they answered seconds, days or the ordinal value, while the same functions over a typed temporal path were refused; such a value is now refused with `400`, as the RM defines `+` for relative amounts and not for dates, times or ordinals.
