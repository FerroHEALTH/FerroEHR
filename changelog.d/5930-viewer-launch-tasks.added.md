- The viewer lists an EHR patient's SMART launch Tasks and launches a configured app from one. With the new `[launch] fhir_base`, the page of an EHR whose subject namespace is one of `launch.patient_namespaces` reads `Task?patient=&code=` at the FHIR facade for the `launch-app-ehr` and `launch-app-standalone` codes, with an access token FerroSMART issues for the facade on the session's grant (RFC 8707 `resource` on refresh, so the sign-in is OpenID Connect over a `memory` session store with `oidc.offline_access`). A Task whose app URL is a configured `[[launch.app]]` `launch_url` gets a Launch button; any other is listed as not configured. A launch reads the Task again, requires its `for` to be the EHR's subject, and either registers the EHR launch at FerroSMART with the Task's patient, encounter and `appContext` or opens the standalone app, always at the configured URL. The viewer now finds FerroSMART's EHR launch API through the `launch_registration_endpoint` of its discovery document.
