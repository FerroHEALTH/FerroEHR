- `ferroehr db sweep-blobs` lists the multimedia blobs no stored version references, and with `--apply` deletes them: the blobs a commit that failed after its upload left in the bucket. A blob younger than the new `[record.multimedia] orphan_grace` (default `"1h"`) is always kept, so an upload whose commit is still running is never deleted, and a commit that stores bytes the bucket already holds renews a blob older than half that period. With offload enabled, an `orphan_grace` of zero is refused at start.
