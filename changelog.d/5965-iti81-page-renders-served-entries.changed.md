- An ITI-81 search page takes its records by their order keys first and renders the document of an access-log entry only when the page serves it, where it rendered every candidate before. A page of 20 renders 20 documents or fewer, and the documents served are unchanged. `audit.access_log_document`, `audit.access_log_instant_text` and `audit.access_log_canonical_uuid` no longer carry a `SET search_path` clause, so the access-log move runs no nested statement per entry (#5963).
