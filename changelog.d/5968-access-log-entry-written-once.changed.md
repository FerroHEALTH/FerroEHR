- An access-log entry is written once. The request writes it into `audit.access_log_entry` with one `audit.access_log_intake` row naming the entries of its statement, and the chainer adds one narrow `audit.access_log_link` row per entry instead of copying the entry into `audit.access_log_record`, which is now the view of the linked entries. Measured with full-page writes off, an entry costs about a third less write-ahead log: 3262 instead of 5045 bytes for a composition read, 2592 instead of 4048 for a single-EHR AQL query, 2007 instead of 3052 per entry of a population query, and 3444 instead of 5328 for a commit. A shared part is now collected by the hourly retention run once its own `retain_until` has passed and no entry names it, in place of an index on every entry's shared digest. Migration `audit/0048` moves the stored entries, their links and any waiting intake rows.
