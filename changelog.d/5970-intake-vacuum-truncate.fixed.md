- Autovacuum no longer truncates the audit intakes (`access_log_intake`, `audit_event_intake`). A truncate took an `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` lock that every request's intake write waited behind, which stalled requests for 110 to 150 ms under load. Migration `audit/0047` sets `vacuum_truncate = off` on both. The operations chapter now recommends `wal_compression = lz4`, and the capacity harness runs with it.
