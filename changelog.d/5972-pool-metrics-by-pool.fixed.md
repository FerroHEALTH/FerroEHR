- The pool metrics name the pool they measure. `db_pool_connections` sampled only the clinical pool. Now it has a series for each of the clinical, party and audit pools and for the audit runtime's own pool. `db_pool_acquire_duration_seconds`, `db_pool_hold_duration_seconds` and `db_pool_nested_acquires_total` carry the same `pool` label, so a wait on the audit pool can be told from one on the clinical pool.
