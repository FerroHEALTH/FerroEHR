- The `openehr-rm` types box their large fields and enum variants, chosen by size in the generator: a polymorphic slot such as `ContentItem` or `Item` is two words (was 5,808 bytes), and a typed read of the 128 KB IPS composition allocates 317 KiB instead of 1,216 KiB and runs about 35% faster. Every RM enum gains `From<payload>` for each variant. This is a breaking change to the published `openehr-rm` API.
