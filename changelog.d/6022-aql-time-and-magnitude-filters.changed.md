- A population AQL query filtered on the time of an event, the origin of a history or the magnitude of an ELEMENT value reads the rows the filter keeps. Such a query used to read every node of the archetype and compare the value last: a worklist of recent lab results (`WHERE o/data[at0001]/events[at0002]/time/value >= $since`) read 26,227 buffers for 20 results of 1,500, and now reads 918. A comparison with `=`, `<`, `<=`, `>` or `>=` on `time/value` of a POINT_EVENT or INTERVAL_EVENT or on `origin/value` of a HISTORY, and one with `<`, `<=`, `>` or `>=` on `value/magnitude` of an ELEMENT, enters through an index when it is a top-level condition of the WHERE clause. The rows a query answers do not change. Migrations `ext/0014_node_magnitude.sql`, `clinical/0053_node_event_time.sql`, `clinical/0054_node_value_magnitude.sql`, `party/0055_node_event_time.sql` and `party/0056_node_value_magnitude.sql` add the column `node.event_time`, fill it for stored rows and build the two indexes; on a large store the fill and the index builds take time in proportion to the node rows. A composition commit writes 1.8% more write-ahead log for them (246,564 bytes against 242,177 on the IPS composition).
