- Every fact an access decision rests on is confirmed by an indexed read in the request, so a change committed on another instance decides the next request here whatever the delay of its notification. A request reads the party store's revisions (grants, caller facts and the binding side table) and the newest versions of the policy registers and the access policy, one statement each, and takes a copy only at the revision it read. Before, a deprovisioned login, a removed membership, a revoked grant and a policy change held on another instance once its notification arrived, usually within milliseconds; care relationships and `EHR_ACCESS` settings were already exact. The party schema gains `binding_grant_revision.binding_revision` (migration `party/0058`), which moves once in each transaction that changes the binding side table. A read sends two more statements than before, and a party commit one more nested statement.
