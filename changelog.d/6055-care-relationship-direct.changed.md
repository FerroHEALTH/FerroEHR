- A care relationship is a `PARTY_RELATIONSHIP` of its own at `/demographic/party_relationship`, named `under_care_of`, whose target is a professional's `PERSON`, a `ROLE`, a care team's `GROUP` or an `ORGANISATION` unit, with a `care_role` and a `provider` in its `details`. A caller holds it directly, by its `PERSON` or a `ROLE` it performs, or through a team or unit it is a member of, and only while it acts for the relationship's provider; the care projection that AQL reads carries the carer and the provider. A relationship without a role or a provider, or with a role outside the bound value set, is refused `422`, and a care relationship held in a `PERSON`'s `relationships` is refused `422` naming the resource (#6055).
