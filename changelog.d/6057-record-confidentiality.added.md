- **A controller can mark a whole record as restricted or very restricted.** The `marking` of an EHR's `ACCESS_DIRECTIVE_SETTINGS` (a `CONFIDENTIALITY_MARKING` of HL7 `HL7_Confidentiality` `N`, `R` or `V`), a label's marking on one object and a classification's `confidentiality` are now stored and decided, where they were refused at write. Step 6 of the decision procedure refuses data above `N` that no applying permit clears by naming that level or a higher one in a data scope's `confidentialities`, with `403 refused-confidentiality` (naming neither level nor reason), or `404` at a level of the policy's `concealed_confidentialities`. AQL leaves such records out inside its one SQL statement, so counts and pages agree, and `GET /ehr?subject_id=` leaves them out. Only the controller sets, changes or clears the marking: a version another gate-keeper commits that changes it is refused `403`. An access to a marked record carries the level as the access-log entry's security label and the BALP `entity.securityLabel`; the controller's commit that sets or clears a marking names the level.
