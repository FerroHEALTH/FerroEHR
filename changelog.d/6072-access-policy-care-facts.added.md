- The access policy stores the controller's facts about care relationships: `self_declared_care`, `attested_care`, `caseload`, `conflict_rules`, `concealed_confidentialities` and `value_sets`, each read at its strictest default when left out. `PUT /ferroehr/rest/extensions/v1/admin/access_policy` validates a policy against the template its `archetype_details.template_id` names, checks each code of a bound attribute against its value set on the terminology server, and refuses a policy that breaks either with `422` naming the attribute (#6072).
