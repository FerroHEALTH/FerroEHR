- The access policy bounds every care relationship written. A relationship written without an end takes the `default_period` of the first `relationship_validity` entry that covers its `care_role` and its provider's organisation type; one longer than that entry's `max_period` is refused `422`, and one no entry covers must be written with an end, so with no policy, or a policy without `relationship_validity`, an open-ended care relationship is refused `422`. The end it takes is kept beside the relationship with the uid of the policy version that gave it, never written into the committed version, and a later policy version applies only to relationships written after it. An `ORGANISATION` target must be a unit `team_targets` names and is never the controller or the relationship's provider. `relationship_validity`, `relationship_writers` and `team_targets` are now stored and read, checked against the draft model's invariants, and their care roles and organisation types against the bound value sets (#6073).
