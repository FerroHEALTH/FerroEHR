- A `write_care_relationship` grant writes only what its elements name: a care relationship needs a `provider` element (a `DV_IDENTIFIER`) naming its provider and a `target_kind` element naming the RM type of its target, and a membership needs a `provider` element naming a provider of the `ORGANISATION` it targets or of the `ORGANISATION` its `GROUP` belongs to. A grant without them writes none (`403`); an administrator still writes every one. A `GROUP` now belongs to an `ORGANISATION` by its own `MBR` relationship, a care-team Group created over SCIM belongs to the provider its client acts for, and a membership of a `GROUP` that belongs to none is an administrator's to write. A caller is never admitted on a care relationship or membership it wrote naming itself; each caller fact and each care projection row names its writer. A `self_declared` care relationship is accepted where the policy sets `self_declared_care`, written by the professional it names and ending within that period, and admits its writer while the policy keeps the switch on. `ferroehr party-binding grant` takes `--provider-issuer` and `--provider-id` for a `provider` element (#6073).
