- The care relationship resources, an extension of ITS-REST under the extensions root: `GET` and `POST` of a subject's care relationships by its subject reference (`/care_relationship?subject_id=&subject_namespace=`) and by its `PERSON` (`/demographic/person/{uid_based_id}/care_relationship`), `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE` of one relationship with `If-Match` and `ETag`, its versioned object, revision history and versions (`/versioned_care_relationship/{relationship_uid}`), and the reverse reading by carer (`/care_relationship?carer=`, with `role`, `path`, `at`, `offset` and `fetch`). Every read is decided per subject it reveals: a relationship is served only to a caller who may read the subject's record, or who writes care relationships for its provider, and each read writes one access-log entry per subject revealed (#6076).
