- An external person admitted under a federation agreement holds a care relationship to a patient when its verified credential attests a current treatment context for that patient (`patient.id`, `trc.id`, `trc.not_before`, `trc.not_on_or_after` in the issuer's `[[auth.federation]]` table) and the access policy's `attested_care` names the agreement by the container uid of its `ROLE`. The relationship is never stored, and the access log names it by the assertion and the agreement. A policy whose `attested_care` entry names an `issuer` other than the partner's federation issuer is refused `422` naming `/attested_care/<n>/issuer` (#6078).
