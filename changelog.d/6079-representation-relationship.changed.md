- **Breaking:** a representation is a `PARTY_RELATIONSHIP` of its own at `/demographic/party_relationship`, named `openehr_access::represents`, whose `details` hold a `basis` (a code of the value set the access policy binds for `representation_basis`, or of `HL7_RoleCode` when it binds none) and a `provider` (a `DV_IDENTIFIER` of type `ORGANISATION`), with an optional `evidence`. One held in a `PERSON`'s `relationships`, or one without a readable `basis` or `provider`, is refused `422`. The default definitions ship `openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-PARTY_RELATIONSHIP.representation.v2.0.0` for it. A representative is admitted only on a representation the CDR stores, live on the day, recorded for the provider `[authz.party_binding.provider]` names and not written by the representative itself; one whose party source reports a representation the CDR does not store is refused `403` with `refused-representation-unrecorded`. The access record of each admitted request names the representation's container, and the access log's `representation` authorisation carries it as its reference (#6079).
- **Breaking:** a representation is written by an administrator, by a caller holding the new `write_representation` grant whose `provider` elements name its provider, or by the patient it represents on the basis `HL7_RoleCode::DELEGATEE` alone, the authorisation of a proxy (EHDS Art. 4(2)). Any other basis written by the patient, and any representation written without such a grant, is refused `403` (#6079).
