- The person an EHR belongs to is read from the party binding. A caller whose authenticated identity binds to the `PERSON` that an EHR's `EHR_STATUS.subject` names is that EHR's subject: they read the record and write its `EHR_ACCESS` settings as the subject, with no role and no purpose of use, and the access log records each access as their own (EHDS Art. 3). A bound person holding no role passes the role gate and reaches only those records. A `persons` login bound to a patient's `PERSON`, one with no professional `ROLE` and no representation, is admitted as that person: the login binding no longer refuses it `refused-wrong-marker`, it holds no role whatever its token asserts, its commits name `PARTY_SELF`, its access record carries the binding `self` with its `PERSON`, and it reaches only the EHRs whose subject is that `PERSON`. A lookup by subject (`GET /ehr?subject_id=…`) judges the EHR it finds. A confined caller (a patient-context launch, a representative, a person with no role) finds only its own, and any other subject answers `404`, as an unknown subject does. Before this, a confined caller's lookup was refused `403`, and FerroSMART's patient picker, whose token carries no role, could not look up the person's own EHR (#6106).
