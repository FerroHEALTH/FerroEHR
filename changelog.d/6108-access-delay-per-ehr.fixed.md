- The EHDS Art. 3(3) access delay follows the party binding. A caller whose authenticated identity is bound to the `PERSON` an EHR's `EHR_STATUS.subject` names reads a held composition of that EHR as absent until a professional releases it: a read answers `404`, a query (pinned to the EHR or across the population) and an export leave it out, a CONTRIBUTION lists none of its versions, and a write to it is refused as a write to an unknown target. This holds whatever roles the caller holds, so a professional reading their own record no longer sees what the delay withholds from them as its person. On every other EHR the caller reads as before. The read of the caller's own subject references for that check is a subject-resolution access record, and every subject-resolution record now names its request (#6108).
