- An archive load (`load_ehrs`) judges each care relationship it lands against the access policy in force, as a write does: one without an end takes the default period of the covering `relationship_validity` entry, counted from its start or its commit date, and its caller fact ends there. One the policy refuses, such as an open relationship no entry covers, is a per-entity failure report and is not loaded, while the rest of the archive loads. Before, a loaded open relationship stayed open (#6123).
