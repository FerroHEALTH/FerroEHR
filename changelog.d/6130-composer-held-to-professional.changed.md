- **A professional's COMPOSITION names the professional as its composer.** A caller bound to a `PERSON` through a token commits a `COMPOSITION` whose `composer` is the placeholder naming the caller, which the server fills with the professional's `PARTY_IDENTIFIED` (the value the committer gets) before the version is stored, or a composer that states nothing the server does not hold. Naming another `PERSON` of the same provider, as a scribe or resident recording for a physician, needs the new `compose_for` grant of the `openehr_access` terminology on a `ROLE` the professional performs, allowed by name in `[authz.grants] allowed`; the committer still names who committed. Any other composer is refused with `403` naming `COMPOSITION.composer`, on create and on modify. The privileged `ROLE` ships as `openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-ROLE.privileged.v1.0.1` with the kind `Compose for`. A committer may now also state the caller's provider identifier. Patients, representatives, system clients, Basic users and unbound callers keep their rules.
