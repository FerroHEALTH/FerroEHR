- **Breaking: `[auth]` names each issuer, and every other key refers to it by that name.** FerroEHR moves to `ferrohealth-smart` 0.32 and `ferrohealth-session` 0.27. An issuer is the table `[auth.issuer.<name>]` (a lower-case letter, then lower-case letters, digits and `_`), the `[[auth.issuer]]` array is gone, and the environment addresses an issuer by its name (`FERROEHR__AUTH__ISSUER__FERROSMART__ISSUER`), never by a position that shifts when an issuer is added. The party, federation and attributes tables sit under their issuer (`[auth.issuer.<name>.party]`, `.federation`, `.attributes`) and name no issuer, and the section-level `[[auth.party]]`, `[[auth.federation]]` and `[[auth.attributes]]` are unknown keys. `[authz.rbac] role_issuers` and `[auth] described_issuer` name tables, and a name no table carries refuses the start, listing the names there are. An issuer's `profile = "…"` is `preset = "…"`, and its profile keys (`token_types`, `required_claims`, `person_key`, `app_only`, `act_represents_patient` and the rest) move into its `[auth.issuer.<name>.profile]` table; `profile_file` loads a profile from a file. The `nl` preset is gone: its mapping is an example profile file in the `ferrohealth-smart` book. Defaults derive: an issuer that names no `audience` takes the openEHR REST API's `baseUrl` under `[server] public_base_url`, the base a SMART app asks for, and `kind` is the preset's (`persons` under `ferrosmart`, `keycloak`, `entra` and `okta`).
- **Breaking: whether an issuer's `patient/` grants count is its profile key `patient_context`**, on under the `ferrosmart` preset and off otherwise, and `[auth.smart] patient_issuers` is removed. Behind FerroSMART, a token of FerroSMART's own patient picker is read as the person who signed in (the preset's `issuer_client_is_person`), so the `AGENT` and the `sign_in_persons` grant a deployment wrote for the picker are no longer needed; the access record still names FerroSMART as the client.
- **Breaking: FerroEHR starts before its authorization servers answer.** Each issuer read from its metadata is read in the background, with a back-off of one second doubling to one minute. Until it is read, its tokens are refused with `503` and the reason `issuer-unread`, `/health/readiness` reports the new `auth_issuers` indicator down, and the served `/.well-known/smart-configuration` answers `503` with `Retry-After`. A `jwks_file` issuer and an introspected issuer are still read at start. Every refusal now names a stable reason code in the `error_description` of its `WWW-Authenticate` challenge (RFC 6750 §3).
- An attested care relationship takes the care role its credential attests in the new federation field `trc.care_role`, one code with its code system, when that role is in the `care_role` value set the access policy binds; otherwise it holds `PART`.
- To move a deployment: rename each `[[auth.issuer]]` table to `[auth.issuer.<name>]`, move its `[[auth.party]]`, `[[auth.federation]]` and `[[auth.attributes]]` tables under it without their `issuer` key, rename `profile` to `preset` and move the profile keys under `profile`, replace the URLs in `role_issuers` and `described_issuer` with table names, move `patient_issuers` to `patient_context = true` on each issuer's profile (FerroSMART needs nothing), and rewrite `FERROEHR__AUTH__ISSUER__<n>__…` and `FERROEHR__AUTH__PARTY__<n>__…` variables under the issuer's name. The Helm chart 40.0.0 takes `config.auth.issuer` as a map by name (a major bump: a list is refused). The book's "Authentication & access" page has the keys.
