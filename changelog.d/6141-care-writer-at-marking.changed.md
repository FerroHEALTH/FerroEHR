- **A care relationship to a marked record needs a writer the access policy names for its level.** When the subject's record is marked `R` or `V` (the highest marking of every EHR whose subject reference names the relationship's source `PERSON`), only a writer an `ACCESS_POLICY.relationship_writers` entry names for that level writes the relationship, the administrator included; any other write is refused `403`, naming the level and the missing entry. The markings are read exactly before the commit takes its connection.
