- **A care relationship to a marked record needs a writer the access policy names for its level.** When the subject's record is marked `R` or `V` (the highest marking of every EHR whose subject reference names the relationship's source `PERSON`), only a writer an `ACCESS_POLICY.relationship_writers` entry names for that level writes the relationship, the administrator included; any other write is refused `403`, naming the level and the missing entry. The markings are read exactly before the commit takes its connection. Such an admitted write is recorded for review: its access-log record carries the level as the new `review` detail (`id40` of `openEHR-EHR-ITEM_TREE.access_log_details.v1.0.0`), the `review` column of `audit.audit_event` and `audit.access_trail`, and a `review` detail on the object of the BALP `AuditEvent` and the DICOM audit message.
