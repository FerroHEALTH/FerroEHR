- **Breaking: nothing FerroEHR writes or sends is named after the product.** The access log details in every `ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY.other_details` are kept under the published, shipped archetype `openEHR-EHR-ITEM_TREE.access_log_details.v1` (stored at boot with the other default definitions, its digest in `deploy/definitions/published.sha256`), their nodes the id-codes `id11` to `id39` in place of `at0001` to `at0029`; `audit.access_trail` reads them by those codes. `ferroehr party-binding grant` writes the client's `AGENT` under the shipped `openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-AGENT.system.v1` and the grant under the new shipped `openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-ROLE.grant.v1`, a policy kind validated against its own template, in place of `ferroehr_client` and `ferroehr_grant`. `[api] system_id` has no product default: unset, it is the host of `[server] public_base_url`, a `production` deployment with neither refuses to start, and a `sandbox` stamps `localhost`. The committer of a commit no caller made is named by the `system_id`, a Basic user's login is issued by the new `[api] basic_issuer` (default `system_id`) in place of `ferroehr`, `[audit] source_id` defaults to the `system_id`, and the new `[audit] role_code_system` (default `system_id`) names the code system of a caller's roles in the DICOM audit message in place of `urn:ferroehr:role`. The viewer names a Basic session's login under the new `[cdr] basic_issuer`, which must equal the CDR's `[api] basic_issuer`. The Compose quickstart, the Helm values and the hosted sandbox use `urn:example:` identifier issuers, and the Helm production overlay sets `[api] system_id`. Nothing reads the old values: v6 starts from a fresh database. `scripts/checks/neutral-identifiers.sh` refuses an archetype id, template id, URN or issuer naming the product in non-test source and the shipped definitions.
