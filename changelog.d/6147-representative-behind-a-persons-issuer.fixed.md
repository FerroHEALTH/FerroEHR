- A representative's login at a `persons` issuer, such as FerroSMART's picker or a SMART app the CDR grants `act_for_persons`, binds as a representative when their `PERSON` carries the representative marker alone. They hold no role and reach a subject's record only while a representation the CDR stores names them for that subject: the subject of the token's launch EHR, else of the EHR the request names, else the subject an EHR lookup by subject names. Any other request answers `403` `refused-representation-unrecorded`. Such a login was refused `403` `refused-wrong-marker` on every request, so FerroSMART's picker failed for a representative.
