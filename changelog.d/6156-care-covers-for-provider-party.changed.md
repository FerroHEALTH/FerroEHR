- A care relationship whose `provider` names no `ORGANISATION` of the store, or whose `covers_for` names no party of its kind, is now refused `422` naming the element, on create and on update. `covers_for` is read in the provider's form: its `issuer` the namespace of the carer's reference, its `id` the carer's container and its `type` the carer's kind (`PERSON`, `ROLE`, `GROUP` or `ORGANISATION`), all three required, and it may not name the relationship's own target.
