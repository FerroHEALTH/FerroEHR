- **A representative or a gate-keeper who is not the subject reads a record's access settings without its confidentiality marking.** The subject and the controller still read the `EHR_ACCESS` settings whole. The other gate-keepers get them without `marking` on `GET /ehr/{ehr_id}/ehr_access`, the `versioned_ehr_access` reads, a resolved CONTRIBUTION and an EHR Extract (unless an `export_ehr_extract` grant covers the EHR), each such version without its `signature` and the response carrying `Redacted: marking`; in AQL a whole `EHR_ACCESS` or its `settings` comes without the marking.
