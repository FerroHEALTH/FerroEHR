- The access log names the care relationship an access rested on in one of two forms. A stored relationship is an `ACCESS_STORED_CARE_GROUND` (its carer, version, grace, cover and provider), and one a verified credential attests is an `ACCESS_ATTESTED_CARE_GROUND` with its path, role, assertion (`id`, `issuer`) and agreement (the container uid of its `ROLE` and the partner's federation issuer). Both carry the care role into the FHIR `AuditEvent` `agent.role` and, new, the DICOM message as a `RoleIDCode`, and a projection written from the log now keeps that role and the attested agreement's version as the live record does. An access that a controller rule's exception admits through care records the same ground. In the draft access-log model, `ACCESS_CARE_GROUND` becomes the abstract parent of the two forms (#6173).
