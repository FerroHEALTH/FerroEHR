- A system audit record is written once. The request writes it into `audit.audit_event` with one `audit.audit_event_intake` row naming the records of its statement, and the chainer adds one narrow `audit.audit_event_link` row per record (position, predecessor digest, digest) instead of copying the whole record out of the intake. The chain digest is the same bytes over the same record, and the verifier, the seals, the retention gaps and the ITI-81, syslog and FHIR Feed output are unchanged. A record is never updated: the `delivered_syslog_at` and `delivered_fhir_feed_at` columns are removed, since a record's row in `audit.fhir_feed_outbox` or `audit.syslog_outbox` is its delivery state, and with them `audit.enqueue_fhir_feed_backlog` and `audit.fhir_feed_backlog`, which entered records written before the outbox existed. A record the intake still names is in no read and no verification until it is linked.
