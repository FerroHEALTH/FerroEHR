- The access-log intake no longer takes a row lock on the shared part of each entry it writes. A part the statement carries is stored or raised by an upsert. The retention sweep removes an orphaned shared part one day after its `retain_until`, where it removed it on that day, so an intake statement that read the part before midnight finds it after. An intake that has to raise a part the sweep is removing is refused as a serialization failure and written again, and no entry ever names a part the store does not hold.
