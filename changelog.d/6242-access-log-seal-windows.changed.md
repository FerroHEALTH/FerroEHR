- An access-log entry is written once, by the request that made the access, and read, sealed and delivered from that row. The per-entry hash chain is gone: no intake row, no link row, no chain head and no per-round checkpoint. A background round about once a second moves the log's frontier, the highest entry id below which every entry is committed or never will be, and about once a minute the entries below it are sealed in windows of at most 100,000 entry ids. A seal is the SHA-256 of a length-prefixed text that starts with the version tag `ferroehr.access_log.seal.v1` and names the window, the previous seal and each entry's id and content digest, and it is signed as the `ACCESS_LOG_CHECKPOINT` of the log up to the window's last entry. The verifier recomputes every window and reports a record changed, removed or inserted inside a sealed window. The fold removes a sealed window once all of its records are tombstones past their end. The draft `ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY` and `ACCESS_LOG_TOMBSTONE` no longer carry a `link` member, `ACCESS_LOG_LINK` is gone, and AQL over the access log has no `link` paths. `GET /admin/access-log/chain` is replaced by `GET /admin/access-log/seals` (`from_id`, `to_id`). One EHR's log is exported as a bundle signed whole by the exporting system, carrying each record's content digest and nothing of another person, and the import records the bundle's digest in its own access-log entry.
