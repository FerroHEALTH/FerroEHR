- The syslog route and the FHIR Feed deliver both audit logs from the store by a cursor per sink and log in place of a row per record in `audit.fhir_feed_outbox` and `audit.syslog_outbox`, which are gone with `audit.access_log_owe` and its table. No record is lost or skipped; after a crash a sink repeats at most the record it had in flight. A record a sink cannot render or its repository refuses is parked: counted in `atna_audit_parked_total`, logged at `ERROR` by its id, shown by the `audit_delivery` indicator as `DEGRADED`, and kept until an operator retries or dismisses it through the new `GET /admin/audit-delivery`, `POST /admin/audit-delivery/retry` and `POST /admin/audit-delivery/dismiss`. Retention removes or tombstones a record a configured sink has not delivered only a day after its end, and parks it then. A new sink is owed every record written from the start of the first server configured with it; a sink taken out of the configuration keeps its cursor and parked records, is named at `WARN` at start, and is removed only by `POST /admin/audit-delivery/forget`.
