- The system audit records (`audit.audit_event`, the records outside the access log) carry no hash chain. A request writes each record once, with a content digest the server computes over every column (form 6, documented beside the verifier), and its position `seq`; the link table, the system intake, the chain head, the chain move, the database row digest and the `audit.verify_audit_chain*` functions are gone. The records are sealed in signed windows as the access log is, under the tag `ferroehr.system_log.seal.v1`, by the same sealer about once a minute; the scheduled five-minute seal of the system chain is gone. `ferroehr audit-chain seal` seals every committed record and prints the newest window's seal, and `ferroehr audit-chain verify` recomputes every window. Retention keeps a reaped record's position and content digest until every record of its sealed window is reaped, then folds the window. `audit_seal_duration_seconds` and `audit_seal_last_success_seconds` now report every sealing of both logs, and the `audit_sender` indicator reads `DEGRADED` once a record has waited three minutes for its seal.
