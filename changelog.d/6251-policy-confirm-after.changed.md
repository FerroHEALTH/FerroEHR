- A read of a COMPOSITION or an EHR no longer makes a statement of its own to
  confirm the policy copies: its own statement returns the policy and
  definition revisions beside the rows it reads, and the answer leaves only once
  the instance's copies held them. A read decided on a copy behind them is run
  once more behind the up-front confirm, and only that answer is served and
  recorded; the new `access_policy_reruns_total` counter counts those runs.
