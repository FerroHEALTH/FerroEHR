- The compose quickstart, its overlays, the hosted sandbox and the Helm chart run the latest releases of the other FerroHEALTH products: FerroPIX 0.0.39 (chart 0.4.3), FerroTERM 0.4.7, FerroBRIDGE 0.0.27 and FerroSMART 0.3.0. Their configurations move with them. FerroPIX names each issuer as a table, `[auth.issuer.<name>]` (`ferropix.config.auth.issuer` is a map by name in the chart), and `[[trust.issuer]] issuer` names that table. The chart writes FerroBRIDGE's `[cdr.endpoint]` and `[terminology.endpoint]`, and refuses `bridge.config.cdr.endpoint` in place of `cdr.base_url` and `cdr.credentials`. FerroSMART's example clients list the resource server they may reach (`resources`), and the ZITADEL upstream drops `protocol`. Chart values that set FerroPIX's issuers as a list, or a FerroBRIDGE `[auth]` with a flat `issuer` and `audience`, move to these shapes.
