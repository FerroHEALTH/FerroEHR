- An access-log entry is written with its key and its uid alone. The columns that the ITI-81 search, the person's trail, the ITS-REST log, AQL over the log and retention filter on now live in `audit.access_log_index`. The audit task fills that table in one batch each frontier round (about once a second), and its cursor commits with the rows. `audit.access_log_record` and `audit.access_log_search` read the indexed entries through it and the entries past the cursor by a key range of their rows, in one statement, so every committed entry is found as before. The entry's own ten indexes become three: the primary key, the `uid` uniqueness (which keeps an erased entry's uid from being written again) and the tombstone fold's, which an entry does not write to. With full-page writes off, an entry writes 614 fewer bytes of write-ahead log on a composition read (1407 instead of 2021), 535 fewer on a single-EHR query, 563 fewer per entry of a population query and 593 fewer on a composition commit, in 3 records where it wrote 8. The verifier also reports an index row that is missing below the cursor, or that differs from its entry.
