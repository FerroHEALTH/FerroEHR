- An ITI-81 `AuditEvent` search reads each access-log entry it serves by its
  key, and takes its candidates' keys from the search index, so a page no
  longer reads the search index and the open window once a record. Where the
  audit store shares the clinical database, the page statement also carries
  the policy and definition revisions, and the search confirms its policy
  copies after it ran in place of a read before it.
