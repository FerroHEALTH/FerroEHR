- An access-log entry is sealed over the facts it stores, never over a rendering of it. Its content digest is now the SHA-256 of the entry's stored form version, its uid, time, EHR, request and resource ids, its shared part's digest and its row part. The verifier recomputes that digest from the row, and checks that each shared part still hashes to the key the entry names it by. Each entry keeps the version of its stored form in the new `form` column. A reader renders the entry's canonical form, its BALP `AuditEvent`, its DICOM message and its EHDS access record by the renderer of that version, and refuses a version it has no renderer for. The documents a reader gets are unchanged. The export still names each entry by the SHA-256 of its canonical form, computed when it is exported. An entry no longer repeats the origins its only item holds as `other_details` elements `id28` and `id29`, as the details archetype says: the count stays when it differs from the item's. The access trail reads both from the item. An only item's categories are stored in the shared part, beside the category content they are classified from. A composition read's entry writes 592 fewer bytes of write-ahead log (815 instead of 1407), a single-EHR query's 156 fewer (915 instead of 1071), a population query's 24 fewer per entry (833 instead of 857), and a composition commit's 312 fewer (1028 instead of 1340). The ITI-81 page and the FHIR Feed no longer read four digest columns nothing used.
