- The draft access control and access log packages describe episodes of care. The care relationship element `context` is now `episode`, the episode of care the relationship is scoped to, in the `openehr_access` terminology and in the shipped `openEHR-DEMOGRAPHIC-PARTY_RELATIONSHIP.under_care_of.v2.0.0` archetype. The draft adds the `record placement element` group, the refusal `refused-outside-episode`, `ACCESS_DATA_SCOPE.episodes`, `ACCESS_POLICY.episode_shared`, `ACCESS_RELATIONSHIP_VALIDITY.episode_required` and `ACCESS_STORED_CARE_GROUND.episode`, which `openehr_rm::draft` carries. No access decision reads them yet.
