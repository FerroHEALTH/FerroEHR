- A care relationship can name the episode of care it is scoped to, with an `episode` element holding the episode's `DV_IDENTIFIER`. FerroEHR keeps the episode with the relationship's caller facts and in the care projection, refuses `422` an episode without an issuer and an id, and refuses `422` a relationship without one where the access policy's `relationship_validity` entry sets `episode_required`. A composition placed in an episode that no care relationship of its EHR's subject names is refused `422` on every write path: a commit, a CONTRIBUTION, an EHR Extract import and an archive load. No access decision reads the episode yet.
