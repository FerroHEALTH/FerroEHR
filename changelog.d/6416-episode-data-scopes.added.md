- A directive, a controller rule, a classification and a delegation name the data of episodes of care with `ACCESS_DATA_SCOPE.episodes`, each a `DV_IDENTIFIER` compared by issuer and id against the episodes a composition's current version is placed in. A patient's restriction of one episode hides that episode's data on a named read and in every query, and nothing else. Settings that differ only in the episodes they name share one settings class.
