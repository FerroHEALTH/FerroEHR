- With `[auth.smart] episode_launch` on, the `episodeId` of a SMART on openEHR launch narrows every request of the session: read as `issuer|id`, it lets the app read and query that episode's data, the data placed in no episode and the data `ACCESS_POLICY.episode_shared` names, and answers a composition of another episode `403` with `refused-outside-episode`; a launch in an episode no live care relationship of the caller names is refused `400`. The flag no longer only advertises the capability.
