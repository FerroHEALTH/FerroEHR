- **Breaking: a care-team membership is coded `openehr_access::member_of`.** FerroEHR read a membership from any party relationship coded `HL7_RoleClass::MBR`, so a deployment's own archetype that used the generic HL7 code got FerroEHR's membership rules: a standalone relationship, a membership held by an `AGENT`, a `GROUP`'s membership of anything but an `ORGANISATION`, or a target outside the store was refused `422`, and writing one needed the `write_care_relationship` grant. A relationship coded `HL7_RoleClass::MBR` is now the client's own content and takes no access meaning. FerroEHR's membership is the new `member_of` term of the draft `openehr_access` terminology, with `HL7_RoleClass::MBR` as its broader mapping on the draft Terminology page, and keeps every rule. The shipped `membership`, `member_of` and `care_team` definitions, the SCIM care-team writer and the party baseline's `binding_caller_fact.kind` use the new code.
