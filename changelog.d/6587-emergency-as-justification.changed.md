- **Breaking:** an emergency is now an emergency justification of the controller's `ACCESS_POLICY` (`justifications` with `emergency: true`), invoked by the callers it names with a declared purpose and a stated reason. It lifts what the break-glass role lifted (each directive, rule and exclusion whose `emergency_override` holds, the opt-out for an access service the agreement lets override, and steps 6 to 8), refuses an emergency without a reason `refused-reason-missing` before any directive is read, and marks only an access that rested on it. The `audit_event.emergency_access` column is replaced by `justification` (`{"kind", "reason"}`, in the record's content digest), the access-log search parameter `emergency` by `justification=justification|emergency`, and the draft access log's `ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY.is_emergency` by the `justification` and `emergency` authorisations with `ACCESS_AUTHORISATION.reason`.
