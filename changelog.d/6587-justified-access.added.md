- Justified access outside the care relationships: the controller's `ACCESS_POLICY.justifications` name the purposes of use (HL7 v3 `ActReason`, such as `TREAT` for a consultation or a second opinion, `ETREAT` or `BTG` for an emergency) and the callers that may invoke each; a caller outside its care relationships is admitted on one when the request states a reason in the `x-access-reason` header (`[audit] reason_header`), percent-encoded UTF-8 of 1 to 1000 characters, and is refused `refused-reason-missing` without one. The access record keeps the purpose and the reason, the access log names the justification with its `reason`, and a patient's restriction still refuses (EHDS Art. 8, GDPR Art. 18).
