- The compose files, the hosted sandbox, the Helm chart and the deployment probes run the newest release of each family product, pinned by digest: FerroPIX 0.0.43 (with its chart 0.5.5), FerroTERM 0.4.8, FerroBRIDGE 0.0.29 and FerroSMART 0.8.0. The chart takes the major version 43.0.0, because FerroPIX 0.0.40 and later refuse the `kind`, `preset` and `profile` keys of a `ferropix.config.auth.issuer` entry and require `identifier_type` on every `ferropix.config.domain` entry; FerroBRIDGE 0.0.29 refuses `kind` (other than `federation`) on a `bridge.config.auth.issuer` entry. Every development issuer signs FerroPIX's client credentials tokens as RFC 9068 `at+jwt`. Behind FerroSMART, `docker/ferrosmart/ferrosmart.toml` names its signing keys in `private_key_files`, sets `assurance_minimum` and configures FerroSMART's own Backend Services client (`[ferrosmart.backend]`, with the `backend_key.pem` that `secrets.sh` writes), and the new `ferrosmart-clients` one-shot registers the clients with `ferrosmart client apply`: `docker/ferrosmart/clients/example-app.json` and the viewer's document, which `docker/ferrosmart/secrets.sh` writes as `viewer_client.json` in place of `viewer.jwks.json`.
