- The viewer's new **Roles and grants** page lists, for an administrator, every role holding an `openehr_access` grant with its grant codes, its terms, the party it is held by, its validity and whether it is in force, and every system agent with its client id, its issuer and the provider it acts for. It reads them through AQL, so the CDR's policy-party rule decides what is shown. A party page now marks a policy party and shows the grants its roles carry ([Access control](https://ferroehr.eu/docs/latest/viewer/access-control.html#roles-and-grants)).
