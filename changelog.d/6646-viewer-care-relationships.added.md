- The viewer shows and manages care relationships. An EHR's new **Care** tab lists its subject's care relationships, the live ones by default and the ended ones on request, each with its carer, care role, period and path. A party's **Care** page shows the same for a `PERSON` and lists the subjects in a carer's care. A relationship's page shows its versions with the audit details of each. A user the CDR lets write care relationships adds one, changes its role or period, ends it today and deletes one written in error, each with `If-Match` and each refusal drawn in the CDR's words ([Access control](https://ferroehr.eu/docs/latest/viewer/access-control.html#care-relationships)).
