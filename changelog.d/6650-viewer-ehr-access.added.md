- An EHR's new **Access settings** tab in the viewer shows its `EHR_ACCESS` settings drawn by directive (kind, effect, what it covers, whom it names, its period and whether an emergency overrides it), says when no settings apply, and lists every version, any of which opens read-only. An administrator commits new settings on the person's instruction, naming the version they replace, or repairs settings that do not parse, each refusal in the CDR's words ([Access control](https://ferroehr.eu/docs/latest/viewer/access-control.html#an-ehrs-access-settings)).
