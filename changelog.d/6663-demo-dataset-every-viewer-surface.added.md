- The synthetic demo dataset now fills every viewer page. One seed (`scripts/sandbox/reseed.sh` and its manifest) loads it into the hosted sandbox every night and into the browser journeys' stack, through the public API alone. It writes patient `PERSON`s with their record subjects, the provider organisation, care teams, a system client and the `ROLE`s that grant, representations, care relationships, the access policy in two versions with a justified and an emergency access between them, EHR access settings, a restriction of processing, a research objection, an access delay, retention periods with something due, attested versions, ADL 1.4 archetypes and a cohort query. On the sandbox, two more demo logins join `ferroehr`: `ferroehr-clinician` and `ferroehr-proxy`. In the journeys' lane, versions are signed with ML-DSA-65+Ed25519 under a committed test key, and the access log is sealed into a signed checkpoint that verifies. The sandbox and development postures admit the access-control grants the dataset writes; the production defaults are unchanged.
