- The viewer's sealed session store is removed: the viewer keeps its sessions in its own process only, the configuration page no longer shows a session key, and the hosted sandbox viewer runs on the memory store. `session.store = "sealed"`, `session.key` and `session.key_file` refuse the start. The viewer is a demonstration of the CDR and runs as one instance: a restart signs everyone out, and more than one replica needs sticky sessions at the ingress.
