- The CONTRIBUTION reads and creates of the EHR and demographic APIs, and the four `VERSIONED_PARTY` reads, now answer `Accept: application/xml` in canonical XML of the ITS-XML types. A CONTRIBUTION is served under a `<contribution>` root, a `VERSIONED_PARTY` under `<versioned_party>`, its revision history under `<revision_history>`, and a party version under the published `<version>` root. Under the stable profile a version carries the RM 1.1.0 `PARTY` with its `reverse_relationships`. A CONTRIBUTION is still committed as JSON only, and a read with `Prefer: resolve_refs` is still served as JSON only.
