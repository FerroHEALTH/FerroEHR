- Post-quantum JOSE signatures (RFC 9964). `[auth] post_quantum_only = true` in `ferroehr.toml` verifies access tokens and DPoP proofs with ML-DSA-44, ML-DSA-65 or ML-DSA-87 alone: every issuer `algorithms` list and `[auth.dpop] algorithms` left at the default becomes the three ML-DSA parameter sets, and a list naming another algorithm stops the start. The viewer's `[auth] post_quantum_only = true` accepts a sign-in ID Token signed with ML-DSA alone; with it off, the viewer accepts ML-DSA beside the classical algorithms. The viewer signs its SMART launch client assertion with an ML-DSA key's parameter set (it took only RS384 and ES384 before), and `docker/ferrosmart/secrets.sh` makes the viewer's launch key ML-DSA-65 with an `AKP` key in its client document. The post-quantum inventory states what `ferrohealth-smart` 0.35.0 accepts and signs on each JOSE row, counts those rows in the post-quantum configuration, and cites RFC 9881 and draft-ietf-tls-mldsa-06 for the TLS certificate row.
