- A cohort predicate now reaches the EHR of each record subject a selected `PERSON` declares, as well as the EHR whose `EHR_STATUS` names the party, and an EHR reached both ways counts once. The installed EHR index provider resolves each record subject as it resolves a named subject, so a predicate cohort also runs under an IHE patient identity source, where it was refused before: a record subject in another domain is translated forward by the PIX Manager, and no pseudonym is ever translated back. The demo dataset's postures bind the `sex` predicate to the seed's ADL 2 patient template, and the seed runs a cohort of its female patients.
