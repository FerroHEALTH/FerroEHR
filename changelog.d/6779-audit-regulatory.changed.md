- `[audit] legal_basis`, a free-text string, is replaced by `[audit.regulatory]`, the typed declaration of ehds-logging 0.30: `legal_basis = { lawfulness = "<GDPR Art. 6(1) point>", condition = "<Art. 9(2) point>" }`, `controllers` and `processors`. Every access record stores the typed legal basis, and the FHIR `AuditEvent` writes it with the controllers and processors on its categories entity (`ehds-legal-basis`, `ehds-legal-condition`, `ehds-controller`, `ehds-processor`, Xt-EHR D5.2 §7.2.7). A configuration that still sets `[audit] legal_basis` is refused as an unknown key; move the basis into `[audit.regulatory]`.
