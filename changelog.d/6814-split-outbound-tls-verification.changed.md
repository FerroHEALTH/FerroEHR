- FerroEHR and the viewer move to the FerroHEALTH family set on `ferrohealth-server` 0.14: `ferrohealth-config` 0.5, `ferrohealth-telemetry` 0.6, `ferrohealth-usage-report` 0.6, `ferrohealth-smart` 0.38.2, `ferrohealth-session` 0.36, `ihe-atna` and `ehds-logging` 0.32, `ihe-iti` 0.30, `ferrobridge-term` 0.25 and `fhir-types` 0.5. Every outbound TLS table now verifies a peer by the root its chain ends at. A chain to a root of `trust_roots_file` is verified by webpki on every operating system, ML-DSA certificates included, and a connection opens only once a check of the chain's CRL or OCSP sources cleared it: the check runs at the start and every 4 minutes, and it fails closed when no source answers. This covers the ITI-20 syslog repository, the OTLP collector, the issuers of `[auth]`, every outbound integration and the viewer's CDR, provider and SMART launch connections. Any other chain goes to the platform's verifier. A table that names a private CA with no CRL or OCSP responder in `trust_roots_file` now refuses the connection: name that CA in `trust_roots_without_revocation_file`.
