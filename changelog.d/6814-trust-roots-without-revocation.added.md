- Every outbound TLS table of FerroEHR and the viewer takes `trust_roots_without_revocation_file`, a PEM bundle of trust roots for a private PKI that publishes no revocation service. A chain to one of them is verified by webpki with no revocation check, and the start names those roots in the banner and in a `WARN` line. The compose overlays and the viewer's end-to-end setup name their development CA there.
