- The Helm chart takes `caRevocation` beside each CA it mounts: `service.tls.caRevocation`, `viewer.auth.oidc.caRevocation` and `auth.caRevocation`. `required`, the default, names the CA in `trust_roots_file`, so a connection opens only once a CRL or OCSP check cleared the chain. `none` names it in `trust_roots_without_revocation_file`, for a CA that publishes no revocation service. A cert-manager CA issuer publishes no CRL, so an in-cluster CA from it needs `none`. The chart moves to 43.1.0.
