h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESS_ACTOR_SCOPE*

h|*Description*
2+a|A description of callers. A caller matches when it matches every attribute that is set.

h|*Attributes*
^h|*Signature*
^h|*Meaning*

h|*0..1*
|*party*: `xref:{class_party_ref}[PARTY_REF]`
a|A party in the demographic service: a `PERSON` or `AGENT` matches itself; an `ORGANISATION` or `GROUP` matches every caller that acts for it or is a member of it.

h|*0..1*
|*role*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|A functional role the caller holds, coded as the role's capability or the caller's token states it, such as a professional role code.

h|*0..1*
|*relationship*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|A relationship the caller must hold to the subject of the EHR, coded in the `access relationship` group: `care` (a live care relationship), `representative` or `proxy`.

h|*0..1*
|*caller_kind*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|The kind of caller, coded in the `access caller kind` group: `professional`, `access_service`, `subject`, `representative` or `system`.

h|*Invariants*
2+a|__Not_empty__: `party /= Void or role /= Void or relationship /= Void or caller_kind /= Void`

h|
2+a|__Relationship_valid__: `relationship /= Void implies terminology (Terminology_id_access_control).has_code_for_group_id (Group_id_access_relationship, relationship.defining_code)`

h|
2+a|__Caller_kind_valid__: `caller_kind /= Void implies terminology (Terminology_id_access_control).has_code_for_group_id (Group_id_access_caller_kind, caller_kind.defining_code)`
