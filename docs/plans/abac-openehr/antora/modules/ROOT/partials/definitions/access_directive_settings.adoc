h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESS_DIRECTIVE_SETTINGS*

h|*Description*
2+a|The concrete `ACCESS_CONTROL_SETTINGS` of the access directive scheme, carried by `EHR_ACCESS._settings_` and versioned with the EHR. It records the facts an access decision about one EHR rests on: the default disposition, the gate-keepers who may change the settings, the directives the subject or their representative gave (consent, restriction, opt-out, delegation scope), and sensitivity labels on individual versioned objects. It carries no rule language: the decision procedure that reads these facts is fixed by the scheme. `EHR_ACCESS.scheme()` returns `access_directives` for an instance of this class.

a|What the EHR admits where no directive applies, coded in the `access default` group: `open` (any caller the other layers admit), `restricted` (only callers a permit directive, a gate-keeper or a care relationship names) or `consent_required` (only callers a permit directive names, apart from the subject and an emergency override).

h|*0..1*

a|The parties who may commit a new version of the `EHR_ACCESS` that holds these settings: usually `PARTY_SELF` for a competent adult, otherwise a parent, guardian or other responsible party referenced into the demographic service (BASE Architecture Overview, Security, Access Control of access settings). Empty leaves the change to the system's administrators.

h|*0..1*
|*directives*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_directive_class[ACCESS_DIRECTIVE]>`
a|The directives in force or recorded for this EHR. A directive outside its `_time_validity_` is kept as history and decides nothing.

h|*0..1*
|*labels*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_label_class[ACCESS_LABEL]>`
a|Sensitivity labels on individual versioned objects of the EHR, beside the labels the system's `ACCESS_POLICY` classification gives whole kinds of data.
