h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESS_POLICY*

h|*Description*
2+a|The access policy of a system or of one controller in it: facts that hold for every EHR the controller keeps. It classifies kinds of data into sensitivities, states whether clinical access needs a care relationship and a declared purpose, lists the named directives a subject can accept by reference, and states what the subject's view of the access log withholds. Kept in its own versioned container, outside any EHR and outside the demographic service.

h|*Inherit*
2+|`xref:common:archetyped_package.adoc#_locatable_class[LOCATABLE]`
