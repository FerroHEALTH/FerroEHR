= The access_control Package

include::partial$draft_notice.adoc[]

== Overview

An `ACCESS_DIRECTIVE_SETTINGS` is the `_settings_` of one `EHR_ACCESS`, and `EHR_ACCESS.scheme()` returns `access_directives` for it. It holds the default disposition of the EHR, the gate-keepers, the `ACCESS_DIRECTIVE` instances the subject gave and the `ACCESS_LABEL` instances on single objects. An `ACCESS_POLICY`, kept in a `VERSIONED_ACCESS_POLICY`, holds the controller's facts. `ACCESS_ACTOR_SCOPE` and `ACCESS_DATA_SCOPE` describe callers and data, and are used by directives, labels and classifications alike.

== Design

=== The Default and the Gate-keepers

`_default_access_` answers for an access no directive decides, in the `access default` group: `open`, `restricted` or `consent_required`. This is the "sensible default" of the Security Policy, set per EHR. `_gate_keepers_` names the parties who may commit the next version of the `EHR_ACCESS`: usually `PARTY_SELF`, otherwise a parent, guardian or other responsible party referenced into the demographic service (Security Policy, "Access control of access settings").

=== Directives

An `ACCESS_DIRECTIVE` is one instruction about access to the data of the EHR. `_kind_` says what it is (`consent`, `restriction`, `opt_out`, `delegation`) and `_effect_` whether it permits or denies. It applies to an access when every attribute it sets matches the access:

* `_applies_to_`: the callers, as `ACCESS_ACTOR_SCOPE` instances, any one of which is enough. A scope names a demographic party (a `PERSON` or `AGENT` matches itself; an `ORGANISATION` or `GROUP` matches whoever acts for it or is a member), a functional role, a relationship to the subject (`care`, `representative`, `proxy`) or a kind of caller.
* `_operations_` and `_purposes_`: the operations and the purposes of use, coded in the terminologies the system documents and accepts.
* `_data_`: the data, as `ACCESS_DATA_SCOPE` instances: single versioned objects, templates, archetypes (with their specialisations), categories, sensitivities, origins, and the interval in which the data were committed.
* `_time_validity_`: when the directive is in force, to the second.

An empty list applies to everything of its kind, so a directive with no `_data_` covers the whole EHR, including data recorded after it. A directive outside its `_time_validity_` is kept as history and decides nothing.

`_exceptions_` holds narrower directives of the opposite effect, one level deep, such as "deny mental health data, except to my GP". `_emergency_override_` states whether a declared emergency lifts the directive (EHDS Art 11(5) for a restriction; Art 10(2) for an opt-out where national law allows it). `_instructed_by_`, `_instructed_`, `_legal_grounds_`, `_evidence_` and `_identifiers_` record who gave the instruction, when, on which legal ground, where the signed record of it sits (the `ADMIN_ENTRY` of the consent, RM EHR IM Entry Package), and its identifiers in other systems. `_details_` carries the deployment's archetyped detail, which the procedure never reads.

The kinds carry the rights of the subject:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
|Kind |Use

|`consent`
|A permit or deny the subject gives: share with a named party or organisation, or refuse one.

|`restriction`
|A deny of professionals' access to all or part of the record (EHDS Art 8). The data read as absent, with no trace of the restriction.

|`opt_out`
|A deny of the whole record to the access services (EHDS Art 10). At most one per EHR.

|`delegation`
|The scope of what a proxy or representative may read (EHDS Art 4(2)), narrowing the relationship the demographic service records.
|===

=== Labels and Classifications

`ACCESS_LABEL` puts sensitivity codes on one versioned object of the EHR. `ACCESS_CLASSIFICATION`, held by the policy, gives every datum a scope describes the same codes: "psychiatric notes are `PSY`". A classification names no single object. The codes come from the terminology the jurisdiction names, such as the HL7 v3 confidentiality and sensitivity codes, so the privacy levels are "defined by standards or agreements within jurisdictions of use" (Security Policy, Privacy).

=== The Controller Policy

`ACCESS_POLICY._controller_` names the `ORGANISATION` whose policy it is. `_care_relationship_required_` makes a professional's clinical access depend on a live care relationship to the subject, and `_relationship_grace_` extends the relationship after it ends for follow-up (Security Policy, "Time-limitation of access"). `_purpose_required_` makes a declared purpose of use mandatory. `_catalogue_` lists named directives a subject accepts by an identifier, such as an IHE BPPC policy or a national consent choice; accepting one copies its directive into the subject's settings, with the signed acknowledgement as `_evidence_`.
