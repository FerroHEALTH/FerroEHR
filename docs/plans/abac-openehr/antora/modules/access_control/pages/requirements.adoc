== What openEHR Asks For

The openEHR specifications name the slot this package fills and the properties a scheme in it should have. Each row is a requirement and the text it comes from.

openEHR gives no concrete settings class, no consent model, no home for a policy that holds for every EHR of a controller, and no authorisation meaning for a demographic `CAPABILITY`. This package supplies the first three. The last stays outside the RM: a system documents the codes its capabilities carry.

== Legal Requirements

Regulation (EU) 2025/327 (EHDS) gives the natural person rights that an access control scheme in the Union must carry:

* *Art 8:* the right to restrict the access of health professionals and healthcare providers to all or parts of the person's data, where the fact of the restriction is not visible to healthcare providers.
* *Art 11(5):* access to restricted data where necessary to protect the vital interests of the person, logged.
* *Art 10:* where a Member State's law gives it, the right to opt out from access through the electronic health data access services, reversible, with Art 10(2) admitting access for vital interests where that law allows it.
* *Art 4(2):* authorisation of other natural persons, for a limited or unlimited period and if needed for a specific purpose only, and the acts of legal representatives under national law.
* *Annex II 3.4:* access rights that take into account the origins and categories of the data.

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) Art 6(1) and 9(2) name the grounds of the processing. Care runs on Art 9(2)(h); explicit consent (Art 9(2)(a)) and vital interests (Art 9(2)(c)) are other grounds a directive or an override can rest on, so the model records the legal ground of each directive.

ISO 22600-1 and -2:2014 describe, by their published scope, policy agreements between domains and formal policy, role, authorisation and delegation models. The package names its three sets of facts in those terms: the subject's policy per EHR, the controller's policy, and the role and delegation model in the demographic service.
