h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESSOR*

h|*Description*
2+a|Who accessed: the party, the healthcare provider they acted for, their roles and groups, the credential they presented, and the chain of clients and parties that acted between them and the system.

h|*Attributes*
^h|*Signature*
^h|*Meaning*

h|*1..1*
|*kind*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|The kind of accessor, coded in the openEHR Terminology `accessor kind` group: a person, a person through a delegated client, the subject of care, a representative, a person of another organisation, an organisation, or a system acting for nobody.

h|*1..1*
|*party*: `xref:common:generic_package.adoc#_party_proxy_class[PARTY_PROXY]`
a|The party that accessed: `PARTY_IDENTIFIED` with an `_external_ref_` to the demographic party the system bound the caller to, `PARTY_SELF` for the subject of care, `PARTY_RELATED` for a representative, or `PARTY_IDENTIFIED` with identifiers alone where no party is known here.

h|*0..1*
|*provider*: `xref:common:generic_package.adoc#_party_identified_class[PARTY_IDENTIFIED]`
a|The healthcare provider or other organisation the accessor acted for.

h|*0..1*
|*roles*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]>`
a|The functional or security roles the accessor held for the access.

h|*0..1*
|*groups*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:{class_party_ref}[PARTY_REF]>`
a|The demographic `GROUP` parties, such as care teams, whose membership the access relied on.

h|*0..1*
|*binding*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|How the system bound the credential to `_party_`, or why it could not, coded in a terminology the system names.

h|*0..1*
|*credential*: `xref:access_log:access_log_package.adoc#_access_credential_class[ACCESS_CREDENTIAL]`
a|The credential the accessor authenticated with.

h|*0..1*
|*delegation*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_log:access_log_package.adoc#_access_credential_class[ACCESS_CREDENTIAL]>`
a|The clients and parties that acted between the accessor and the system, outermost first, as the credential named them.
