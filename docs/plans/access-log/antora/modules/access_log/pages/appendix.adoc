== Conformance Points

[cols="1,4", options="header"]
|===
|Point |A conforming system

|AL-1 Recording
|writes entries by xref:semantics.adoc#_recording[Recording] and serves the full view to reviewers.

|AL-2 Integrity
|chains every record by xref:semantics.adoc#_integrity[Integrity] and serves `chain` and `checkpoint`.

|AL-3 Retention
|states and applies retention by xref:semantics.adoc#_retention[Retention].

|serves the subject view through `/ehr/{ehr_id}/access_log`.

|exports and imports an EHR's records with their checkpoint.

|supports `ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY` as an AQL root.

== Relation to Other Audit Formats (informative)

An entry projects onto the records other standards define. The projection writes what the target can hold and loses the rest:

* *FHIR `AuditEvent` under IHE Basic Audit Log Patterns 1.1.4:* the action, times, outcome, purposes, accessor, provider, credential, client, items, query, request id and system map to `action`, `recorded`, `period`, `outcome`, `purposeOfEvent`, `agent`, `entity` and `source`. The order of the delegation chain, the legal grounds, the origins, the retention and the hash chain have no element. `_ehr_id_` is carried as an identifier, since BALP expects a `Patient` reference.
* *DICOM PS3.15 A.5 audit message:* the action, outcome, accessor and items map to `EventIdentification`, `ActiveParticipant`, `AuditSourceIdentification` and `ParticipantObjectIdentification`, with 110101 "Audit Log Used" for a read of the log (A.5.3.2). Purposes, authorisations and the emergency mark have no element in the A.5.1 schema.
* *EHDS Annex II 3.2:* (a) `_accessor.provider_`, (b) `_accessor.party_`, (c) `_items.categories_`, (d) `_time_recorded_`, (e) `_items.origins_`.
* *NEN 7513:2024 (Netherlands):* the DICOM-derived fields map as above; `participantSegments` maps to `_items.paths_`, `behandelrelatieProtocol` and `toestemmingsProfiel` to `_authorisations_`, and the list of checks the access decision ran (`controles`) goes to `_other_details_` under a national archetype.
