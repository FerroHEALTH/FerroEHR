= Requirements

== Overview

The model answers, for every access to EHR data, the questions below. Each row names the requirement and the published source it comes from.

[cols="1,3,2", options="header"]
|===
|Question |Requirement |Source

|Who
|The provider or other individuals, and the specific natural person.
|Regulation (EU) 2025/327 (EHDS) Annex II 3.2(a), (b); ISO 27789:2021, scope (the user)

|On whose behalf
|The delegation between the person and the system, and the representation a representative acts under.
|ISO 22600-2:2014, scope (delegation model); RFC 8693 §4.1 (`act`)

|What
|The EHR and the data accessed, with the categories and the origins.
|EHDS Annex II 3.2(c), (e); Art 9(2)(c)

|When
|The date and time of access.
|EHDS Annex II 3.2(d); Art 9(2)(b)

|Why
|The purpose of use.
|DICOM PS3.15 A.5 ("for what action"); FHIR `AuditEvent.purposeOfEvent` (R4), `authorization` (R5)

|Under which authorisation
|The policy, agreement, consent decision or permit.
|ISO 22600-1:2014, scope (policy agreements); IHE BALP 1.1.4 3:5.7.6

|Outcome
|Granted or refused, with the reason.
|DICOM PS3.15 A.5.1 `EventOutcomeIndicator`

|System and transport
|The recording system, the interface, the request.
|DICOM PS3.15 A.5.1 `AuditSourceIdentification`, `NetworkAccessPointID`; IHE BALP 1.1.4 3:5.7.3.1
|===

== Legal Requirements

Regulation (EU) 2025/327 makes the log a requirement for EHR systems in the Union. Annex II 3.2 asks the logging component to record, "on every access event or group of events", (a) the healthcare provider or other individuals, (b) the specific natural person, (c) the categories of data, (d) the time and date and (e) the origins. Annex II 3.3 asks for review tools, Annex II 3.4 for retention periods and access rights that take the origins and categories into account, Art 9 for the person's right to read who accessed their data for at least three years from each access, and Art 11(5) for an emergency access to restricted data to be logged and easily accessible to the person.

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 applies to the log itself: Art 5(1)(c) and (e) bound what an entry holds and how long it is kept, Art 30 has the controller record the log among its processing activities, and Art 32 asks for the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the log and weighs unauthorised access to it.

National law adds to this. In the Netherlands, the Besluit elektronische gegevensverwerking door zorgaanbieders, Art 5(1), requires the logging of a care information system to meet NEN 7513, and Art 5(2), with the Besluit vaststelling bewaartermijn logging, keeps it at least five years from the moment a log line is written. NEN 7513 specifies the events logged (access to patient data, access to the logging, and events that can affect the meaning or reliability of the logging) and the data of each log line. The Wabvpz, Art 15e, gives the client a copy of who viewed or requested which information, and when.
