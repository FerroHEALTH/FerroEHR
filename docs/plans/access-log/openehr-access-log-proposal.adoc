== Purpose and motivation

The openEHR Architecture Overview (BASE, Security and Confidentiality,
§Security Policy) lists access logging among the measures every EHR
deployment needs and states: "Currently openEHR does not specify models of
such logs, but might do so in the future." The same section names record
demerging as a use of the log: "the access logs for that EHR should be used
to determine who has accessed that data".

openEHR audits every change (`AUDIT_DETAILS` on each `CONTRIBUTION` and
`VERSION`), and no read. Implementations log reads in foreign formats (IHE
ATNA, FHIR `AuditEvent`) or in their own, so a log cannot be read in openEHR
terms, queried with AQL, or moved with an EHR from one CDR to another.

Regulation (EU) 2025/327 (EHDS) makes the log a legal requirement for EHR
systems in the Union: Annex II 3.2 lists what each record must identify,
Annex II 3.4 asks for retention per category and origin, and Art 9 gives
each person the right to read who accessed their data, for at least three
years from each access.

This proposal adds:

. an RM package, `org.openehr.rm.access_log`, defining the log's records;
. five openEHR Terminology groups for its coded attributes;
. the rules for writing, chaining, retaining and reading the log;
. ITS-REST resources to read it;
. an AQL root class for review queries.

It changes no existing class.

== Requirements

The model answers, for every access to EHR data:

[cols="1,3,2"]
|===
|Question |Requirement |Source

|Who
|The provider or other individuals, and the specific natural person.
|EHDS Annex II 3.2(a), (b); ISO 27789:2021 scope (the user)

|On whose behalf
|The delegation between the person and the system, and the representation
a representative acts under.
|ISO 22600-2:2014 scope (delegation model); RFC 8693 §4.1 (`act`)

|What
|The EHR and the data accessed, with the categories and the origins.
|EHDS Annex II 3.2(c), (e); Art 9(2)(c)

|When
|The date and time of access.
|EHDS Annex II 3.2(d); Art 9(2)(b)

|Why
|The purpose of use.
|DICOM PS3.15 A.5 ("for what action"); FHIR `AuditEvent.purposeOfEvent` (R4), `authorization` (R5)

|Under which authorisation
|The policy, agreement, consent decision or permit.
|ISO 22600-1:2014 scope (policy agreements); IHE BALP 1.1.4 3:5.7.6

|Outcome
|Granted or refused, with the reason.
|DICOM PS3.15 A.5.1 `EventOutcomeIndicator`

|System and transport
|The recording system, the interface, the request.
|DICOM PS3.15 A.5.1 `AuditSourceIdentification`, `NetworkAccessPointID`; IHE BALP 1.1.4 3:5.7.3.1
|===

== Placement

The log is a separate, append-only store of the EHR system, one per
`system_id`, keyed by `ehr_id`. It is not EHR content. Reasons:

* EHR content is indelible (BASE §Security Policy, "Indelibility"); log
  entries expire (EHDS Art 9(2); Regulation (EU) 2016/679 Art 5(1)(e)).
* Recording a read as versioned content would make every read a commit.
* An entry names staff and organisations, which would otherwise travel with
  every EHR Extract.

The package sits in the RM beside `ehr`, `demographic` and `ehr_extract`
and depends on `common.generic` (`PARTY_PROXY`, `PARTY_IDENTIFIED`,
`PARTY_RELATED`), `data_types` and `support.identification`.

== Terminology

Five groups are added to the openEHR Terminology, with constants on
`OPENEHR_TERMINOLOGY_GROUP_IDENTIFIERS` (`Group_id_access_action`,
`Group_id_access_outcome`, `Group_id_accessor_kind`,
`Group_id_access_authorisation`, `Group_id_access_refusal_reason`). The
codes are for the terminology editors to assign; the rubrics are:

[cols="2,5"]
|===
|Group |Rubrics

|access action
|read, query, create, update, delete, export, import

|access outcome
|granted, partially granted, refused, failed

|accessor kind
|person, delegated person, subject of care, representative, external person,
organisation, system

|access authorisation
|role, agreement, consent, permit, emergency, subject of care,
representation, legal obligation

|access refusal reason
|not authenticated, credential not bound, not authorised, consent absent,
opted out, restricted by the subject, rate limited, system failure
|===

`access refusal reason` is recommended; a system MAY code
`ACCESS_OUTCOME.reason` in its own terminology.

== Recording

. A system MUST write an `ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY` for every access to EHR data
through any of its interfaces: reads, queries, creates, updates, deletes,
exports and imports, whether granted, refused or failed.
. An access that reaches the data of several EHRs MUST be recorded as one
entry per EHR whose data it asked for or delivered. The entries of one
request MUST carry the same `channel.request_id`.
. A system MUST write the entry, or a durable reservation of it, before it
returns EHR data to the accessor, and MUST refuse the access when it cannot.
. A read of an access log MUST itself be recorded, with an
`ACCESSED_ITEM` whose `object` names the log.
. An entry MUST NOT carry data values of the EHR. An item names objects and
versions; a query is named by `stored_query` or `text_digest`.
`ACCESS_QUERY.text` MAY be recorded where a policy requires it.
. An entry MUST NOT carry a credential; `ACCESS_CREDENTIAL` names it.
. `accessor.party` SHOULD reference the demographic party the system bound
the caller to. Where no party is known, it MUST carry the identifiers the
credential gave.
. A record MUST NOT be modified after it is written, except by its
replacement by a tombstone (<<Retention>>).

[[Integrity]]
== Integrity

. The canonical form of a record is its ITS-JSON serialisation with the
`link` attribute removed, canonicalised as the JSON Canonicalization Scheme
of RFC 8785.
. `content_digest` is the digest of the UTF-8 octets of the canonical form.
. `digest` is the digest of the UTF-8 octets of `previous_digest`, a line
feed, the decimal `sequence`, a line feed, and `content_digest`.
. The first record of a log has `sequence` 1 and a `previous_digest` the
system publishes with its first checkpoint.
. Every record of one `system_id` MUST carry the next `sequence`, without
gaps.
. A system SHOULD write an `ACCESS_LOG_CHECKPOINT` at intervals its policy
sets and on request, and SHOULD deposit checkpoints where the recording
system cannot alter them.
. A verifier recomputes `digest` for each record from `previous_digest`,
`sequence` and `content_digest`, recomputes `content_digest` for each
`ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY`, and checks the last digest against a checkpoint. A
tombstone's `content_digest` cannot be recomputed and is taken as recorded.
. A system MAY sign each link (`ACCESS_LOG_LINK.signature`).
. A change of algorithm takes effect at a stated sequence; each link names
its algorithm.

[[Retention]]
== Retention

. An entry MUST state its retention when written: `retain_until` and the
grounds that set it, from the categories and origins of the data and the
law that applies.
. At the end of the period, the system MUST replace the entry by an
`ACCESS_LOG_TOMBSTONE` with the same `uid`, `system_id`, `time_recorded` and
`link`. An erasure ordered by law before then takes the same path.
. A store that receives entries from another system MUST keep their
`retain_until` and MAY extend it.

== Reading

=== Views

A system serves two views of an entry:

* **The full view**, to a party authorised to review the log.
* **The subject view**, to the subject of care of the EHR and to their
  representative. It MUST include `time_recorded`, `occurred`, `action`,
  `outcome.result`, `accessor.kind`, `accessor.party`, `accessor.provider`,
  `items` (without `origins.endpoint`), `purposes` and `is_emergency`. It
  MUST omit `accessor.credential`, `accessor.delegation`, `accessor.binding`,
  `channel`, `query.text`, `query.text_digest`, `security_labels`,
  `other_details` and `link`. A system MAY withhold further attributes
  where the law of the jurisdiction restricts the subject's right to them.

The caller's authorisation selects the view. A request parameter MUST NOT
widen it.

=== ITS-REST resources

[cols="2,3"]
|===
|Resource |Behaviour

|`GET {baseUrl}/ehr/{ehr_id}/access_log`
|The entries of one EHR, newest first. Query parameters: `from`, `to` (ISO
8601 date-times), `action`, `outcome`, `accessor_kind`, `emergency`,
`include_self`, `offset`, `fetch`. `include_self` defaults to false and
leaves out the subject's own reads of their log.

|`GET {baseUrl}/ehr/{ehr_id}/access_log/{uid}`
|One entry or tombstone.

|`GET {baseUrl}/admin/access_log`
|Entries across EHRs, for review. Query parameters: `ehr_id`,
`party_namespace`, `party_type`, `party_id`, `issuer`, `subject`,
`request_id`, `from`, `to`, `outcome`, `emergency`, `offset`, `fetch`.

|`GET {baseUrl}/admin/access_log/chain`
|The `uid` and `link` of each record between `from_sequence` and
`to_sequence`.

|`GET {baseUrl}/admin/access_log/checkpoint`
|The latest checkpoint, or the one at `sequence`.

|`GET {baseUrl}/admin/access_log/export?ehr_id=`
|The records of one EHR with the checkpoint they verify against, for a
transfer of the EHR to another system.
|===

A list response is a JSON object with `offset`, `fetch` and `entries`, each
entry in its ITS-JSON form with `_type`. A request for an EHR the caller may
not see MUST be answered as for an EHR that does not exist (`404`). Every
read of these resources is itself recorded.

=== AQL

`ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY` MAY be used as a top-level class expression in the
`FROM` clause:

----
SELECT l/time_recorded, l/accessor/party/external_ref/id/value, l/items/version_id/value
FROM ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY l[ehr_id/value=$ehr_id]
WHERE l/outcome/result/defining_code/code_string = $granted
----

It MUST NOT appear inside a containment of `EHR` or of any class that holds
EHR content, so a query cannot return log entries and clinical data in one
result. Querying the log requires its own permission, and each such query
is recorded.

== Relation to other audit formats (informative)

An entry projects onto the records other standards define. The projection
writes what the target can hold and loses the rest:

* **FHIR `AuditEvent` under IHE Basic Audit Log Patterns 1.1.4:** the action,
  times, outcome, purposes, accessor, provider, credential, client, items,
  query, request id and system map to `action`, `recorded`, `period`,
  `outcome`, `purposeOfEvent`, `agent`, `entity` and `source`. The
  delegation chain's order, legal grounds, origins, retention and the hash
  chain have no element. `ehr_id` is carried as an identifier, since BALP
  expects a `Patient` reference.
* **DICOM PS3.15 A.5 audit message:** the action, outcome, accessor and
  items map to `EventIdentification`, `ActiveParticipant`,
  `AuditSourceIdentification` and `ParticipantObjectIdentification`, with
  110101 "Audit Log Used" for a read of the log (A.5.3.2). Purposes,
  authorisations and the emergency mark have no element in the A.5.1 schema.
* **EHDS Annex II 3.2:** (a) `accessor.provider`, (b) `accessor.party`, (c)
  `items.categories`, (d) `time_recorded`, (e) `items.origins`.

The FerroEHR design document (`docs/plans/access-log.md`) holds the
attribute-by-attribute tables.
