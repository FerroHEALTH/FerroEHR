|===
h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESS_ACTOR_SCOPE*

h|*Description*
2+a|A description of callers. A caller matches when it matches every attribute that is set.

h|*Attributes*
^h|*Signature*
^h|*Meaning*

h|*0..1*
|*party*: `xref:{class_party_ref}[PARTY_REF]`
a|A party in the demographic service: a `PERSON` or `AGENT` matches itself; an `ORGANISATION` or `GROUP` matches every caller that acts for it or is a member of it.

h|*0..1*
|*role*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|A role the caller performs: the caller matches when it is the `performer` of a `ROLE` in force (its `time_validity` holds the date of the access and its versioned object is not deleted) whose `name` (`PARTY.type()`) is a `DV_CODED_TEXT` with the same terminology and code, or when the verified credential of a caller from outside the system, admitted under an agreement, attests a professional role with the same terminology and code.

h|*0..1*
|*relationship*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|A relationship the caller must hold to the subject of the EHR, coded in the `access relationship` group: `care` (a live care relationship, narrowed by `_care_roles_` and `_care_path_`), `representative` or `proxy`.

h|*0..1*
|*care_roles*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]>`
a|The roles in a care relationship the scope names: a caller matches when it holds a live care relationship to the subject whose `care_role` has the terminology and code of one of them. Void means any role. No role implies another.

h|*0..1*
|*care_path*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|How the care relationship reaches the caller, coded in the `care path` group: `direct`, `team`, `attested` or `self_declared`. Void means `direct` or `team`, and `attested` or `self_declared` only where the policy admits that path.

h|*0..1*
|*organisation_type*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|A type of organisation the caller acts for: the caller matches when it acts for an `ORGANISATION` whose `name` (`PARTY.type()`) is a `DV_CODED_TEXT` with the same terminology and code, or when the verified credential of a caller from outside the system, admitted under an agreement, attests a type of its healthcare facility with the same terminology and code.

h|*0..1*
|*caller_kind*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|The kind of caller, coded in the `access caller kind` group: `professional`, `access_service`, `subject`, `representative` or `system`.

h|*Invariants*
2+a|__Not_empty__: `party /= Void or role /= Void or organisation_type /= Void or relationship /= Void or caller_kind /= Void`

h|
2+a|__Relationship_valid__: `relationship /= Void implies terminology (Terminology_id_access).has_code_for_group_id (Group_id_access_relationship, relationship.defining_code)`

h|
2+a|__Caller_kind_valid__: `caller_kind /= Void implies terminology (Terminology_id_access).has_code_for_group_id (Group_id_access_caller_kind, caller_kind.defining_code)`

h|
2+a|__Care_roles_valid__: `care_roles /= Void implies (not care_roles.is_empty and relationship /= Void and then relationship.defining_code.code_string.is_equal ("care"))`

h|
2+a|__Care_path_valid__: `care_path /= Void implies (relationship /= Void and then relationship.defining_code.code_string.is_equal ("care") and terminology (Terminology_id_access).has_code_for_group_id (Group_id_access_care_path, care_path.defining_code))`
