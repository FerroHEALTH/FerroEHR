h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESS_CONFLICT_RULE*

h|*Description*
2+a|A conflict of interest the controller allows through the professional path, for the callers named. An access admitted under it is marked in the access log.

h|*Attributes*
^h|*Signature*
^h|*Meaning*

h|*1..1*
|*conflict*: `xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]`
a|The conflict, coded in the `access conflict` group: `self` (the caller is the subject) or `related` (the caller is related to the subject).

h|*0..1*
|*applies_to*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_actor_scope_class[ACCESS_ACTOR_SCOPE]>`
a|The callers the allowance covers. Void means every professional.

h|*0..1*
|*kinships*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]>`
a|For `related`, the kinship codes the allowance covers. Void means every kinship.

h|*Invariants*
2+a|__Conflict_valid__: `terminology (Terminology_id_access).has_code_for_group_id (Group_id_access_conflict, conflict.defining_code)`

h|
2+a|__Applies_to_valid__: `applies_to /= Void implies not applies_to.is_empty`

h|
2+a|__Kinships_valid__: `kinships /= Void implies (not kinships.is_empty and conflict.defining_code.code_string.is_equal ("related"))`
