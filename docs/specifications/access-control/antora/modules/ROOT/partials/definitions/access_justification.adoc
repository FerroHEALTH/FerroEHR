h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESS_JUSTIFICATION*

h|*Description*
2+a|A ground on which the controller admits a caller to an EHR outside its care relationships: a purpose of use the caller declares, with a reason it states, such as a consultation, a second opinion or an emergency. An access that rests on it is marked in the access log with the reason.

h|*Attributes*
^h|*Signature*
^h|*Meaning*

h|*1..1*
|*purposes*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:data_types:text_package.adoc#_dv_coded_text_class[DV_CODED_TEXT]>`
a|The purposes of use, such as the `HL7_ActReason` codes `TREAT`, `ETREAT` or `BTG`, whose declaration invokes the justification.

h|*0..1*
|*applies_to*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_actor_scope_class[ACCESS_ACTOR_SCOPE]>`
a|The callers that may invoke the justification. Void means every caller of kind `professional`.

h|*1..1*
|*emergency*: `xref:{class_boolean}[Boolean]`
a|Whether the justification is an emergency: it then lifts each directive, rule and exclusion whose `_emergency_override_` holds and the steps on sensitivity, confidentiality, care relationship and default, and the access is marked as an emergency access (EHDS Art. 11(5)). Otherwise it stands in for a live care relationship alone.

h|*Invariants*
2+a|__Purposes_valid__: `not purposes.is_empty`

h|
2+a|__Applies_to_valid__: `applies_to /= Void implies not applies_to.is_empty`
