h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESS_POLICY*

h|*Description*
2+a|The access policy of a system or of one controller in it: facts that hold for every EHR the controller keeps. It classifies kinds of data into sensitivities, states whether clinical access needs a care relationship and a declared purpose, holds the controller's own rules on which callers may perform which operations on which kinds of data, lists the named directives a subject can accept by reference, states what the subject's view of the access log withholds, and states how long the access log keeps an entry. Kept in its own versioned container, outside any EHR and outside the demographic service.

h|*Inherit*
2+|`xref:common:archetyped_package.adoc#_locatable_class[LOCATABLE]`

|*relationship_grace*: `xref:data_types:date_time_package.adoc#_dv_duration_class[DV_DURATION]`
a|How long after a care relationship ends it still admits access, for follow-up (BASE Architecture Overview, Security, Time-limitation of access). Grace never extends a team membership.

h|*0..1*
|*time_zone*: `xref:{class_string}[String]`
a|The time zone, as an IANA time zone name, in which the dates of a relationship's `time_validity` are read. Void means UTC.

h|*0..1*

a|The purposes of use for which a care relationship is a ground. Void means the `HL7_ActReason` codes `TREAT`, `ETREAT` and `CAREMGT`. An access that declares another purpose is not admitted by a relationship.

|*relationship_writers*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_relationship_writer_class[ACCESS_RELATIONSHIP_WRITER]>`

h|*0..1*
|*team_targets*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:{class_party_ref}[PARTY_REF]>`

h|*0..1*

a|The longest period of a self-declared care relationship. Void means a self-declared relationship is refused at write.

h|*0..1*
|*attested_care*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:data_types:basic_package.adoc#_dv_identifier_class[DV_IDENTIFIER]>`
a|The agreements under which a caller's verified credential may attest a care relationship. Void means no attested relationship is admitted.

h|*0..1*

h|*0..1*
|*conflict_rules*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_conflict_rule_class[ACCESS_CONFLICT_RULE]>`
a|The conflicts of interest the controller allows through the professional path. Void allows none: a professional who is the subject, or is related to the subject, is refused.

h|*0..1*

a|The confidentiality levels whose refused records are answered as absent. Void answers every refused named record as forbidden.

h|*0..1*
|*value_sets*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_value_set_class[ACCESS_VALUE_SET]>`
a|The value sets the controller binds the coded attributes of care relationships, representations and rules to, held on a terminology server. A binding left out uses the default value set of the package.

h|*1..1*
|*purpose_required*: `xref:{class_boolean}[Boolean]`
a|Whether an access to clinical data must declare a purpose of use. An access without one is refused before the controller's rules are read, whatever rules the policy holds.

h|*0..1*
|*rules*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_directive_class[ACCESS_DIRECTIVE]>`
a|The controller's rules for every EHR it keeps: which callers may perform which operations on which kinds of data, such as the rules a Member State lays down under EHDS Art. 11(3). Each is an `ACCESS_DIRECTIVE` of kind `controller_rule`. A deny rule that applies refuses whatever the subject's directives permit; a permit rule acts only on sensitivity, and a permit exception only narrows the deny rule it sits in.

h|*0..1*
|*catalogue*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_directive_class[ACCESS_DIRECTIVE]>`
a|Named directives a subject can accept by an identifier, such as an IHE BPPC policy or a national consent choice. Each carries at least one identifier.

h|*0..1*
|*log_restrictions*: `xref:{class_list}[List]<xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_log_restriction_class[ACCESS_LOG_RESTRICTION]>`
a|The restrictions a Member State law lays on the subject's view of the access log under EHDS Article 9(3). Each names the kinds of accessor and the attributes of the accessor the subject view withholds; the full entry is unchanged.

h|*0..1*
|*log_retention*: `xref:access_control:access_control_package.adoc#_access_log_retention_class[ACCESS_LOG_RETENTION]`
a|How long the access log keeps each entry, and the legal bounds the controller declares on that period. Void keeps every entry without end.

h|*0..1*
|*details*: `xref:data_structures:item_structure_package.adoc#_item_structure_class[ITEM_STRUCTURE]`
