== Conformance Points

[cols="1,4", options="header"]
|===
|Point |A conforming system

|AC-1 Settings
|stores `CONFIDENTIALITY_SETTINGS` or `ACCESS_DIRECTIVE_SETTINGS` in `EHR_ACCESS._settings_`, checks every version against the invariants and the terminology groups, and refuses a failing version whole.

|AC-2 Decision
|decides every access to an EHR whose scheme is `confidentiality` or `access_directives` by xref:decision_procedure.adoc#_procedure[the procedure], queries included.

|AC-3 Invisibility
|answers an opted-out EHR and restricted data as absent, and never shows the refusal (xref:decision_procedure.adoc#_refusals[Refusals]).

|AC-4 Settings access
|admits reads and writes of the settings by xref:decision_procedure.adoc#_reading_and_writing_the_settings[Reading and Writing the Settings].

|AC-5 Controller policy
|stores `ACCESS_POLICY` as a versioned object and applies its classifications, care relationship, purpose and catalogue, and the restrictions of the access log's subject view.

|AC-6 Exchange
|exports and imports the `EHR_ACCESS` versions in an EHR Extract.

|AC-7 Relationship store
|stores care relationships, memberships, representations and subject facts one by one as versioned objects, addressed by subject reference, and reads them only through the paths of xref:decision_procedure.adoc#_holding_a_care_relationship[Holding a Care Relationship], confirmed current for each decision.

|AC-8 Demographic
|meets AC-7 and also shows the relationships in the parties of its demographic service.

|AC-9 Writers
|admits relationship writes by xref:decision_procedure.adoc#_writing_relationships[Writing Relationships].

|AC-10 Caseload
|narrows a query to the caller's caseload by xref:its_rest.adoc#_queries_and_the_caseload[Queries and the Caseload].

|AC-11 Optional paths
|states which of the paths `self_declared` and `attested` it implements; a path it does not implement matches no caller whatever the policy sets.
|===

== Who Sees What (informative)

One patient, P1, under the care of one hospital, the provider H, under a policy with a `_relationship_grace_` of 30 days and these four rules:

. Clinical access needs a care relationship: `_care_relationship_required_` holds (step 7).
. A record at `R` is read in care, for treatment: a permit rule whose actor scope names `care` and whose data scope names the confidentiality `R`, for the purpose `TREAT`.
. A record at `V` is read only by the responsible or attending practitioner, directly, for treatment: a permit rule naming `care`, the roles `RESP` and `ATND`, the path `direct`, the confidentiality `V` and the purpose `TREAT`, with `_emergency_override_`.
. Only the responsible practitioner signs the discharge summary: a deny rule for writing the discharge summary template, with a permit exception naming `care`, the role `RESP` and the path `direct`.

=== Episodes

P1 is admitted to cardiology and seen by dermatology. The hospital's ADT system issues the episodes `H|C-17` (cardiology) and `H|D-4` (dermatology) and writes the relationships with them; the GP's relationship names none. The policy marks `_episode_required_` for every role of an organisation of type hospital, and its `_episode_shared_` names the patient summary categories.

----
patient:P1#care[ATND]@person:jansen   provider H, episode H|C-17
patient:P1#care[ATND]@person:smit     provider H, episode H|D-4
patient:P1#care[RESP]@person:huisman  provider G (the GP), no episode

The exception is a `consent` permit, since `Restriction_denies` makes every restriction a deny and `Exceptions_opposite` gives an exception the opposite effect of its parent.
