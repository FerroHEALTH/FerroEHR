|0.1.0
|Care relationships with a role, a provider and cover, read through the closed paths `direct`, `team`, `self_declared` and `attested`, each relationship versioned on its own; `_care_roles_` and `_care_path_` on `ACCESS_ACTOR_SCOPE`. `CONFIDENTIALITY_SETTINGS` (scheme `confidentiality`) as the parent of `ACCESS_DIRECTIVE_SETTINGS`, with the marking of a whole record, the controller's exclusions and the order `N` < `R` < `V`; a confidentiality on labels, classifications and data scopes. The controller's facts about care relationships, value-set bindings and a profile as a template of the policy archetype; representations and kinships as stored facts the writer decides. The refusals `refused-confidentiality`, `refused-excluded` and `refused-conflict`. HL7 code systems named `HL7_<name>`. The care relationship resources and the caseload members of a query.

The `_purposes_` of a `controller_rule` match only a purpose the access declares.
