h|*Class*
2+^h|*ACCESS_LOG_LINK*

h|*Description*
2+a|One link of the hash chain of an access log. `_content_digest_` is the digest of the record's canonical serialisation without its link; `_digest_` is the digest over `_previous_digest_`, `_sequence_` and `_content_digest_`, so a removed or reordered record breaks every later link while a tombstone, which keeps the content digest, does not.

h|*Attributes*
^h|*Signature*
^h|*Meaning*

h|*1..1*
|*sequence*: `xref:{class_integer64}[Integer64]`
a|Position of the record in the log of its `_system_id_`, starting at 1, with no gaps.

h|*1..1*
|*algorithm*: `xref:{class_string}[String]`
a|Name of the digest algorithm, such as `SHA-256`.

h|*1..1*
|*previous_digest*: `xref:{class_string}[String]`
a|The `_digest_` of the preceding record, in lower-case hexadecimal; for the first record, the digest the log was started with.

h|*1..1*
|*content_digest*: `xref:{class_string}[String]`
a|Digest of the record's canonical serialisation with `_link_` removed, in lower-case hexadecimal.

h|*1..1*
|*digest*: `xref:{class_string}[String]`
a|Digest over `_previous_digest_`, `_sequence_` and `_content_digest_`, in lower-case hexadecimal.

h|*0..1*
|*signature*: `xref:{class_string}[String]`
a|Optional signature of the recording system over `_digest_`, for a jurisdiction that asks for each record to be signed.

h|*Invariants*
2+a|__Sequence_valid__: `sequence >= 1`

h|
2+a|__Algorithm_valid__: `not algorithm.is_empty`

h|
2+a|__Digests_valid__: `not previous_digest.is_empty and not content_digest.is_empty and not digest.is_empty`
