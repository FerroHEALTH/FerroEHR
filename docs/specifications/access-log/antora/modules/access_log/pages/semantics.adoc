= Recording, Integrity and Retention

== Recording

. A system MUST write an `ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY` for every access to EHR data through any of its interfaces: reads, queries, creates, updates, deletes, exports and imports, whether granted, refused or failed.
. An access that reaches the data of several EHRs MUST be recorded as one entry per EHR whose data it asked for or delivered. The entries of one request MUST carry the same `_channel.request_id_`.
. A system MUST write the entry, or a durable reservation of it, before it returns EHR data to the accessor, and MUST refuse the access when it cannot.
. A read of an access log MUST itself be recorded, with an `ACCESSED_ITEM` whose `_object_` names the log.
. An entry MUST NOT carry data values of the EHR. An item names objects and versions; a query is named by `_stored_query_` or `_text_digest_`. `ACCESS_QUERY._text_` MAY be recorded where a policy requires it.
. An entry MUST NOT carry a credential; `ACCESS_CREDENTIAL` names it.
. `ACCESSOR._party_` SHOULD reference the demographic party the system bound the caller to. Where no party is known, it MUST carry the identifiers the credential gave.
. An event that can change the meaning or the reliability of the log MUST be recorded as an entry whose item names the log: a change to its retention settings, a change of digest algorithm or signing key, a restore from backup, a correction of the system clock, and an erasure ordered by law.
. A record MUST NOT be modified after it is written, except by its replacement by a tombstone (<<_retention>>).

== Integrity

. The canonical form of a record is its ITS-JSON serialisation canonicalised by the JSON Canonicalization Scheme of RFC 8785. Its content digest is the digest of the UTF-8 octets of that form, unless the system stores the record as facts from which a deterministic renderer gives the canonical form back: the content digest is then the digest of those stored facts with the version of the renderer, which the system stores with the record and by which it renders the record for every reader, so a rendering is never what is sealed. A tombstone keeps the content digest of the entry it replaced, which the system stores beside it.
. A system seals its records in windows of consecutive record numbers, each window following the one before it with no number between. A window's seal is the digest of a text that starts with a version tag and names the window, the previous seal and each record's number and content digest in number order, each field as its length in octets, a colon, the field and a line feed.
. A system MUST NOT seal a window while a record whose number falls in it may still be written.
. A system SHOULD sign each seal as an `ACCESS_LOG_CHECKPOINT` of the log up to the window's last record, and SHOULD deposit checkpoints where the recording system cannot alter them.
. A verifier recomputes each window's seal from its records' content digests, recomputes the content digest of each `ACCESS_LOG_ENTRY` from what the system stores of it, follows the seals from one to the next, and checks a checkpoint's signature and seal.
. A change of algorithm or version tag takes effect at a stated window.

== Retention

. An entry MUST state its retention when written: `_retain_until_` and the grounds that set it, from the controller's access policy and the law that binds the controller, and SHOULD name the version of that policy in `_policy_`.
. At the end of the period, the system MUST replace the entry by an `ACCESS_LOG_TOMBSTONE` with the same `_uid_`, `_system_id_` and `_time_recorded_`, keeping the entry's content digest beside it. An erasure ordered by law before then takes the same path.
. A store that receives entries from another system MUST keep their `_retain_until_` and MAY extend it.

== Access to the Log

The log is personal data about the subject of care and about the people who accessed the data, and its processing needs the same security as the EHR.

. Reading the log MUST require a permission granted for the log alone, never one granted with clinical access rights.
. Every read of the log MUST be recorded (<<_recording>>).
. A system SHOULD keep the log in a store whose writers cannot alter or remove its records, and MUST detect such an alteration by the seals (<<_integrity>>).

== Views

A system serves two views of an entry:

* *The full view*, to a party authorised to review the log.
* *The subject view*, to the subject of care of the EHR and to their representative. It MUST include `_time_recorded_`, `_occurred_`, `_action_`, `_outcome.result_`, `_accessor.kind_`, `_accessor.party_`, `_accessor.provider_`, `_items_` (without `_origins.endpoint_`), `_purposes_` and each `_authorisations_` entry of kind `justification` or `emergency` with its `_reason_`. It MUST omit `_accessor.credential_`, `_accessor.delegation_`, `_accessor.binding_`, `_channel_`, `_query.text_`, `_query.text_digest_`, `_security_labels_` and `_other_details_`. A system MAY withhold further attributes where the law of the jurisdiction restricts the subject's right to them.

The caller's authorisation selects the view. A request parameter MUST NOT widen it.

== Exchange

A system that transfers an EHR to another system exports the EHR's records, each entry with the digest of its canonical form and each tombstone with the content digest it keeps, signed whole by the exporting system, and carries no record or digest about another person. The receiving system verifies the signature and every entry's digest, keeps the records' `_system_id_`, `_retain_until_` and content digests, and records the import as an entry of its own log that names the export's digest and a digest over its records.
